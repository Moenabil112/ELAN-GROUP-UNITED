---

### Document Purpose

This checklist (قائمة الرفع) governs how a document is prepared and cleared before it is uploaded to the data room — its name, bundle, format, version, owner, review status, metadata, access level, quality check, and pre-upload approval. It ensures nothing enters the data room unclassified, unversioned, or unreviewed. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

Every document passes a consistent gate before upload: correct naming and format, a confirmed version and owner, complete metadata, an assigned access level, a passed quality check, and an approval to upload. Upload status moves through six labels from **Not Started** to **Uploaded**, with **Needs Update** for revisions. The checklist enforces that classification (Document 02) and versioning (Document 05) are set before a document is visible to any reviewer.

---

### Data Room Reference

Naming convention: ELAN-{DOMAIN}-{TYPE}-v{n} / data-room ID DR-B#-## · access level per Document 02 · version per Document 05 · owner per role · approval before upload.

---

### Purpose of the Upload Checklist

The checklist prevents ungoverned documents entering the data room. It makes upload a controlled step — classified, versioned, quality-checked, and approved — rather than a simple file drop.
