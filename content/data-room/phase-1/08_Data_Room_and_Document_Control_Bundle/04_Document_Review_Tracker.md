### Document Purpose

This tracker (سجل المراجعة) records the review of every data-room document — the reviewer, review type, date, comments, required corrections, approval status, next action, deadline, and escalation if delayed. It is the control that moves documents from draft to review-approved. **All entries are illustrative placeholders to be populated; all figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

Every document is reviewed under one or more of six review types — financial, legal, operational, technical, governance, and language — each by an accountable reviewer, against a deadline, with comments resolved before approval. The tracker makes review status visible and auditable, and defines escalation when reviews slip. It works with the Version Register (Document 05): approved reviews trigger version advancement, and required corrections trigger new draft versions.

---

### Data Room Reference

Review types: financial (FAL) · legal (LCL/counsel) · operational (OAL) · technical (TSA) · governance (RCL/ADM) · language (DKL) · escalation to ES per cadence · aligns with Independent Review Protocol (B5-07).

---

### Purpose of the Review Tracker

The tracker ensures no document reaches reviewers or a decision without documented review. It records who reviewed what, when, with what result, and what must happen next — making the review path auditable and complete.

### Review Points

- No document is review-approved with **open comments**.
- Corrections trigger **new draft versions** (Doc 05).
- Delays **escalate** to ES; counsel blockers tie to LD-14.
