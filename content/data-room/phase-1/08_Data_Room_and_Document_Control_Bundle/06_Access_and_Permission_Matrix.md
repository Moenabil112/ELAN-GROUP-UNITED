### Document Purpose

This matrix (مصفوفة الوصول) defines data-room permissions (صلاحية) by user category — view, download, comment, upload, and approval rights — across document categories, plus access expiry and removal. It operationalizes the classification matrix (Document 02) and the Data Room Access Policy (B7-06). **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; access is NDA-gated and least-privilege.**

---

### Executive Summary

Seven user categories — from Internal Sponsor and Strategic Partner to the Data Room Administrator — hold defined permissions calibrated to their role and to document classification. Most external users view and comment but cannot download sensitive classes or upload; only the administrator uploads, and only accountable humans approve. Access is time-boxed with defined expiry and a removal process, and every action is logged. This matrix is the enforcement layer beneath the classification and access policies.
