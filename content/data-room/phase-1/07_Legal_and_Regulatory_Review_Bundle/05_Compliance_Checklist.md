### Document Purpose

This is a legal and regulatory checklist (قائمة الفحص القانوني والتنظيمي) that organizes the review items to be cleared before any final investment commitment. It is a checklist to structure counsel's work, not a compliance opinion. **This is not legal, tax, or regulatory advice; items marked [Professional Review] / [Licensed Review] / [Missing Input] require qualified advisors; no statute or treatment is asserted here.**

---

### Executive Summary

The checklist spans entity and authority review, investment structure and capital contribution, governance documentation, the financial-model disclaimer, data-room and confidentiality controls, tax/accounting and regulatory placeholders, contract documentation, and advisor sign-off. It reflects the ecosystem's compliance map — some areas active, some assessment-triggered, and several pending input — and it is gated by counsel engagement (LD-14). Its role is to ensure nothing material is unreviewed before commitment.

---

### Legal Review Reference

Compliance map: active (contract, procurement, ABC, COI, AI-governance); assessment (sector/mining-geodata, financial, sanctions, AML/KYC, environmental); pending input (corporate, data-protection, tax/zakat, employment). Counsel pending (**LD-14**). No treatment asserted where context is silent.

> **Legal notice:** Ticking an item means it has been reviewed by a qualified advisor — not that this document provides the review.
