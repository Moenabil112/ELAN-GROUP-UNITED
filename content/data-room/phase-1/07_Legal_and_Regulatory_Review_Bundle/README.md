### Purpose of the Package

This bundle **organizes the legal review path before any final investment commitment**. It provides the confidentiality, term-sheet, structuring, rights, compliance, data-room-access, and professional-review instruments needed to move from interest to a counsel-reviewed decision. It is prepared for readiness for legal-counsel review and conversion into Word or PDF. **This bundle is not legal, financial, or tax advice; every instrument is a non-binding draft requiring licensed counsel [Licensed Review]; governing law is not selected; no regulatory or tax treatment is asserted.**

### Relationship to the Investment Decision Bundle

This bundle is the legal counterpart to the Investment Decision Bundle: it takes the decision framing (SAR 100m / 30% / window / SAR 517m reference) and organizes the confidentiality, term-sheet, structuring, and rights instruments needed to move toward definitive documentation. The Non-Binding Term Sheet here develops the outline in the Investment Decision Bundle.

### Relationship to the Governance & Risk Bundle

The data-room access, decision-rights, and independent-review elements align with the Governance & Risk Bundle: RBAC and classification match the Data Governance Policy; the legal risk (counsel not engaged, LD-14) is the register's top legal item; and the Professional Review Disclaimer formalizes the Independent Review Protocol.

### Relationship to the Data Room

The Data Room Access Policy governs how every bundle's documents are shared under confidentiality, classification, and residency rules; the NDA is the prerequisite for access; and the Professional Review Disclaimer is the standard notice applied across the entire data room.
