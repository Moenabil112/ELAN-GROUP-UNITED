### Document Purpose

This is a **more complete first-draft** non-binding term sheet (مذكرة شروط غير ملزمة) for the proposed strategic entry. It develops the indicative terms into a form advisors can negotiate. It is **non-binding**, creates no obligations, and is subject in every respect to independent review and definitive documentation. **This is not legal or financial advice; all figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

The proposed transaction is a **SAR 100 million** entry (رأس مال الدخول) for a **30% participation** (الحصة التفاوضية) in the **Local Operating Entry Window** (نافذة التشغيل المحلية), priced against a **reference post-money valuation of SAR 517 million** (volumetric reference only). Capital deploys on a **40 / 60** milestone-linked basis; the Strategic Partner receives defined governance and reporting rights and data-room access; and entry is conditional on independent financial, legal, and operational review and definitive documentation. Every term below is indicative and non-binding.
