### Document Purpose

This is a formal professional-review disclaimer (تنبيه المراجعة المهنية المستقلة) to be applied across every document in the ELAN data room. It states that the materials are preparatory, that all figures are illustrative, and that no commitment should be made without independent financial, legal, operational, and technical review. **This document, like the materials it governs, is not legal, financial, or tax advice.**

---

### Executive Summary

Every document in the ELAN packages is a **preparatory, illustrative working draft** intended to support an informed decision — not to conclude one. No figure is a confirmed value or a promise of return; no instrument is executable; no legal, tax, or regulatory treatment is asserted. Before any final investment commitment, the materials must be reviewed by independent financial, legal (licensed counsel), operational, and technical advisors, and the final decision rests with the decision-maker on that advice. This disclaimer is the standard notice that accompanies the entire data room.

### 1. Purpose

To make explicit, across all package documents, that the materials are preparatory and require independent professional review before any reliance or commitment.

### 2. Scope of Review

Independent review spans five domains — financial, legal, operational, technical, and data — plus valuation and model-assumptions review and risk review. No domain may be skipped before commitment.

### 3. Financial Review

An independent financial advisor/auditor should review the model assumptions, staging, channel economics, discount-rate sensitivity, and the basis of the reference valuation. **All financial figures are illustrative and model-derived and are not a promise of return.**

### 4. Legal Review

Licensed counsel (KSA + Morocco — LD-14) should review the entity and participation structure, the IP-core protections (license-not-sale + clawback; no transfer), all contract instruments, governing law, and dispute mechanics. **Nothing in the materials is legal advice, and governing law is not selected.**

### 5. Operational Review

An independent operational reviewer should review the capability build plan, milestones, governance-gate design, and reporting readiness.

### 6. Technical Review

An independent technical/security reviewer should review the data/compute architecture, residency and air-gap controls, access control (RBAC), and field integration.

### 7. Valuation and Model Assumptions

The valuation reference (SAR 517 million) is a volumetric anchor only; the model's computed outputs are illustrative. **Valuation and all model assumptions require independent review and confirmation with real data before any reliance.** Partners are PROPOSED; channels are verifiable, not contracted.

### 8. Final Decision Responsibility

Independent review **informs** the decision; it does not replace the decision-maker's judgment or the governance gates. **The final investment decision, and responsibility for it, rests with the decision-maker**, taken on independent professional advice.

### 9. Standard Disclaimer Text

> **Standard Disclaimer (for use in all package documents):**
> *This document is a preparatory working draft prepared to support an informed decision. It is not legal, financial, tax, or investment advice, and it is not an offer, a solicitation, or a commitment. All figures are illustrative and model-derived, are working assumptions pending real data, and are not a promise of return. All partners are proposed and unconfirmed; all channels are verifiable, not contracted. All instruments are non-binding drafts requiring review by licensed counsel and qualified advisors. Governing law is not selected and no tax, accounting, or regulatory treatment is asserted. No reliance should be placed, and no commitment made, without independent financial, legal, operational, and technical review. The final decision, and responsibility for it, rests with the decision-maker.*
