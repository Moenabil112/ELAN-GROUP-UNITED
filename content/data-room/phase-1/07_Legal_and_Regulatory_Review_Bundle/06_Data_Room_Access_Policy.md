### Document Purpose

This policy governs access (الوصول إلى غرفة البيانات والمستندات) to confidential documents in the data room — user roles, access levels, classification, sensitive-file handling, download restrictions, logging, version control, and the approval/removal processes. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; sovereign-sensitive data is held in-country and never leaves; access is governed by NDA and role-based controls.**

---

### Executive Summary

Data-room access is **role-based and least-privilege**. Five user roles (founder, board, investor, lender, legal) map to defined access scopes; documents are classified into five sensitivity classes; and every access requires an executed NDA plus access tags. Sensitive files carry download restrictions and watermarking; every view and download is logged in an append-only audit trail; and access is approved and removed through defined processes. The policy protects the ecosystem's confidential information and the sovereign-data residency rule throughout diligence.

### Purpose of the Data Room Access Policy

The policy ensures confidential information is disclosed only to authorized users, only to the extent needed, and always under confidentiality and audit — enabling diligence without compromising sensitive information or data sovereignty.

### Sensitive File Handling and Download Restrictions

- **Sensitive file handling:** highest-class files are view-restricted; sensitive-data sharing gated at OG-11.
- **Download restrictions:** downloads restricted or disabled by class; watermarking (role + timestamp) applied; no bulk export of sensitive classes.

### Version Control and Access Removal

- **Version control:** every document version is a new file + manifest entry (never overwrite); naming ELAN-{DOMAIN}-{TYPE}-v{n}.
- **Access removal:** access is removed on completion, withdrawal, or breach; removal is logged; confidentiality obligations survive removal.
- **Confidentiality obligations:** all access is subject to the NDA (Document 01) and survives access removal.
