### Document Purpose

This is a **first-draft** non-disclosure agreement (اتفاقية السرية) template for controlled document sharing ahead of any investment discussion. It is a drafting scaffold with placeholders, not an executable agreement. **This is not legal advice; the template must be completed and reviewed by licensed counsel [Licensed Review] before any use.**

---

### Executive Summary

The NDA governs how confidential information — including the data room (غرفة البيانات والمستندات), the financial model, and business and operational information — is shared with a prospective Strategic Partner (الشريك الاستثماري) and its advisors. It defines confidential information, permitted use, restrictions, data-room access, term, and return/destruction, with legal terms (governing law, jurisdiction) left as placeholders for counsel. Its purpose is to enable diligence while protecting the ecosystem's sensitive information and the protected IP core.

### 2. Purpose — الغرض

To permit the Receiving Party to evaluate a possible strategic participation in the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية) by reviewing confidential information under obligations of confidentiality.

### 3. Confidential Information — المعلومات السرية

Confidential information includes, without limitation: data-room contents; the financial model and its assumptions; business, commercial, and operational information; governance and legal materials; and any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential. **Sovereign-sensitive data remains subject to residency rules and is not exported.**

- **Data room access:** materials accessed via the data room are confidential information.
- **Financial model confidentiality:** the model and all illustrative figures are confidential; figures are illustrative and model-derived.
- **Business and operational information:** operating, commercial, and technical information is confidential.

### 4. Permitted Use — الاستخدام المسموح

The Receiving Party may use confidential information solely to evaluate the potential transaction, and for no other purpose. No license to the protected IP core is granted or implied; the IP core is never transferred.

### 5. Restrictions — القيود

- **Restrictions on use:** use limited to evaluation; no commercial, competitive, or derivative use.
- **Restrictions on disclosure:** no disclosure except to advisors on a need-to-know basis bound by equivalent obligations; no public disclosure.
- No copying beyond what is necessary; no reverse engineering; no circumvention of access controls.

### 6. Data Room Access — الوصول إلى غرفة البيانات

Access is role-based, subject to the Data Room Access Policy (Document 06): access tags, watermarking, download restrictions, and logging apply. Access may be granted, restricted, or removed at the Disclosing Party's discretion.
