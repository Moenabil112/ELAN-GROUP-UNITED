### Document Purpose

This memo sets out possible legal structuring (الهيكلة القانونية) options for the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية), so that counsel and the parties can select and document a structure. It presents options and considerations, not a recommendation. **This is not legal advice; structure selection and all instruments require licensed counsel [Licensed Review]; governing law is not selected.**

---

### Executive Summary

The 30% strategic participation can be instrumented through several structures — a local operating company interest, a joint operating vehicle, or a strategic participation agreement — each documented via a shareholder or participation agreement with defined governance and reporting rights. Across all options, two constraints are fixed: the **protected IP core is never transferred** (held in IP-Co, license-not-sale + clawback), and **operational liability sits in the operating entity, not the IP core**. Structure selection depends on counsel review of corporate, tax, and regulatory factors that are currently open.

### Shareholder Agreement and Rights Path

Whichever structure is chosen, the relationship is documented through a shareholder/participation agreement defining: governance rights (حقوق الحوكمة), reporting rights (حقوق التقارير), information rights (حقوق الاطلاع), reserved matters (المسائل المحجوزة — placeholder), capital-contribution mechanics, and distribution treatment — all **[Licensed Review]**.

### Reporting Obligations and Capital Contribution Mechanics

- **Reporting obligations:** periodic operating and financial reporting per the agreed cadence.
- **Capital contribution mechanics:** SAR 100m contributed on a **40 / 60** milestone-linked basis; contribution, drawdown, and default mechanics **[Licensed Review]**.
