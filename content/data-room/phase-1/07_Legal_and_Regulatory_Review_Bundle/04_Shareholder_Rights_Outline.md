### Document Purpose

This outline sets out the rights (حقوق الشريك الاستثماري) that the Strategic Partner would hold in the Local Operating Entry Window — participation, economic, reporting, governance, information, data-room, capital-review, distribution, and amendment rights. It is an outline for legal drafting, not a definitive rights schedule. **This is not legal advice; all rights require documentation and licensed counsel review [Licensed Review]; reserved matters are placeholders.**

---

### Executive Summary

The Strategic Partner's rights attach to a **30% participation in the window** and fall into economic, reporting, governance, and information categories. The Partner receives a defined economic participation, reporting and information rights, governance visibility with reserved-matter protections (placeholder), data-room access, and capital-deployment review rights — without acquiring the protected IP core or operational control. Every right below is indicative and subject to definitive drafting; reserved matters (المسائل المحجوزة) in particular are a placeholder for counsel and negotiation.

---

### Legal Review Reference

Rights attach to the **window (30%)**, not the ecosystem. IP core never transferred; operational control retained. Reserved matters, veto, and thresholds **[Professional Review]**. All rights **[Licensed Review]**.

---

### Purpose of Shareholder Rights

Shareholder rights define what the Strategic Partner can expect, see, influence, and receive — giving the participation real content while keeping it bounded to the window and preserving founder control of the IP core and operations.

### Information, Data-Room, and Capital-Review Rights

- **Information rights (حقوق الاطلاع):** access to defined operating, financial, and governance information; inspection/audit rights [Professional Review].
- **Data-room rights:** role-based access under the Data Room Access Policy (Document 06).
- **Capital-deployment review rights:** oversight of the six-center deployment against milestones.
