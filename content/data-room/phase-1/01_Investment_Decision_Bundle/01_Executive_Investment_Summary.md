### Document Purpose

This summary opens the Investment Decision Bundle. It gives a senior decision-maker the essential shape of the proposed strategic entry in a single reading: what is being offered, on what basis, what the capital builds, what the Strategic Partner (الشريك الاستثماري) receives, and what must be validated before the file moves to professional review. It is a decision document, not a marketing document. Every figure is illustrative and model-derived, and no figure constitutes a binding offer or a promise of return.

---

### Executive Summary

ELAN Group United is opening a **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** — a defined, bounded participation in the local operating layer of the ecosystem, priced and governed as its own investable unit rather than as an open stake in the whole group.

The proposed transaction is a **SAR 100 million** entry (رأس مال الدخول) in exchange for a **30% participation (الحصة التفاوضية)** in the Local Operating Entry Window. A **reference post-money valuation of SAR 517 million (التقييم المرجعي بعد الاستثمار)** is provided as a volumetric anchor for the wider ecosystem — it frames scale only and is **not** used to recalculate the 30% participation. The investment is assessed over a **15-year horizon**, consistent with the live financial model.

The capital is not a general grant. It converts, on a staged and milestone-linked basis, into measurable local operating capability: the team, the governance layer, the in-country data and compute base, the field-integration layer, the sustainability and impact function, and a protective risk reserve. In return, the Strategic Partner receives a defined 30% economic participation in the operating window, governance visibility, reporting rights, oversight of capital deployment, and controlled data-room access — within a structure where operational control and the protected IP core remain undisturbed.

### How the Capital Is Deployed

Capital is deployed on a **staged 40 / 60 basis**: an initial tranche activates the foundation, team, and governance layer; the balance is released as documented milestones are met and governance gates clear. No center is funded in full up front, and no field-spend phase proceeds without a decision gate. This structure keeps capital exposure proportional to demonstrated progress and preserves a dedicated reserve against delay and execution surprises.

---

### What the Strategic Partner Receives

- A **30% economic participation** in the Local Operating Entry Window.
- **Governance visibility** into the operating window and its decision gates (بوابات الحوكمة).
- **Reporting rights** on operating and financial performance.
- **Oversight of capital deployment** against milestones.
- **Controlled access** to the Data Room (غرفة البيانات والمستندات) for financial, legal, and operational review.

The Strategic Partner does **not** acquire ownership of the protected IP core, and does not acquire control of the operating entity. Participation is a defined economic and governance interest in the operating window — structured, bounded, and reviewable.

### Main Validation Points Before Professional Review

Before the file advances, the following must be independently validated:

1. **Financial framing** — confirmation of the model assumptions, the staging logic, and the basis of the reference valuation. *(Financial Review — المراجعة المالية)*
2. **Legal structuring** — the entity and participation structure, including how the 30% window interest is documented. *(Legal Review — المراجعة القانونية)*
3. **Operational readiness** — the capability build plan, milestones, and the governance gates that release capital. *(Operational Review — المراجعة التشغيلية)*
4. **Data-room completeness** — that the Data Room supports the three reviews above.

All partners and channel assumptions referenced in the model are **proposed**, not agreed, and all figures are illustrative pending confirmation with real data.
