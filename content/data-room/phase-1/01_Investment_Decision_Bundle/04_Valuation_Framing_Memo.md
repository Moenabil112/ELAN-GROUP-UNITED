### Document Purpose

This memo frames the financial logic of the proposed entry. It explains how the **Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول)**, the **30% participation (الحصة التفاوضية)**, the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)**, and the **reference post-money valuation (التقييم المرجعي بعد الاستثمار)** relate to one another; how the entry value connects to operating capability; how the 15-year model supports the discussion; and what must be reviewed before any valuation is confirmed. It is a framing memo, not a valuation opinion. All figures are illustrative and model-derived; none is a binding offer or a promise of return, and none should be treated as a confirmed valuation.

### How the Valuation Reference Supports the Decision Context

The SAR 517 million reference gives the decision a **sense of scale** for the wider ecosystem without importing that scale into the pricing of the window. Its role is contextual: it tells the committee how large the surrounding system is, so the bounded window can be understood in proportion. Keeping the reference separate from the participation calculation protects the decision from two errors — treating the window as if it were the whole ecosystem, and treating a scale anchor as if it were a confirmed transaction price.

### How the Entry Value Relates to Operating Capability

The entry value is carried by **capability, not by paper**. The SAR 100 million converts, on a staged basis, into an operating base — team, governance, in-country data and compute, field integration, sustainability function, and reserve. The valuation logic therefore rests on the proposition that a governed, milestone-linked build produces a measurable operating asset, and that the 30% participation is a claim on the economics of that asset rather than on an abstract enterprise figure.

### How the 15-Year Model Supports Valuation Discussion

The 15-year model supports the valuation discussion by expressing outcomes as **modeled results under stated assumptions across a defined horizon**, not as a single point estimate. It carries scenario logic — conservative, base, and expansion — so the committee can see the sensitivity of value to the primary assumptions rather than a single number. In this framing, the discount rate is treated as the **declared negotiation variable**: valuation methodology is transparent from the outset, and the sensitivity of value to the rate is disclosed rather than hidden.

### Value Creation Logic — منطق خلق القيمة

Value creation is framed as a chain from capital to capability to channels to protected outcomes:

| Link | Contribution to value |
|---|---|
| Revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد) | Five verifiable channels convert capability into recurring and project-based income. |
| Capital deployment (توظيف رأس المال) | Staged, milestone-linked deployment turns capital into a measurable operating base. |
| Governance (بوابات الحوكمة) | Decision gates and separation of duties keep spend proportional to progress. |
| Operating execution | Disciplined execution converts the base into demonstrated channels. |
| Risk controls (سجل المخاطر) | The Risk Register and reserve protect value against delay and downside. |

The channels are presented as **verifiable**, not contracted: no contract is claimed, and channel pricing remains to be defined and confirmed.

### Summary Conclusion

The valuation framing rests on three disciplined separations: the **participation** attaches to the window, not the ecosystem; the **reference valuation** anchors scale, not price; and **value** is carried by staged, governed operating capability, not by an abstract figure. On this basis, the SAR 100 million / 30% entry can be discussed coherently against a SAR 517 million ecosystem reference without conflating the two. No figure here is confirmed; the framing prepares the valuation for professional review, not for adoption.
