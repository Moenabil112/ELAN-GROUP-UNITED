### Document Purpose

This document sets out the structure of the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** — the defined unit into which the Strategic Partner (الشريك الاستثماري) enters. It defines the window, the scope of the 30% participation, its relationship to the broader ecosystem, and the governance, reporting, oversight, data-access, and decision-gate mechanics that frame it. It closes with the future legal-structuring requirements and a review checklist. All figures are illustrative and model-derived; the structure below is indicative and subject to legal structuring and professional review.

---

### Executive Overview

The Local Operating Entry Window is the **local operating layer** of the ELAN ecosystem, ring-fenced and presented as its own investable unit. The Strategic Partner's **30% participation (الحصة التفاوضية)** attaches to this window exclusively. The window is where **Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول)** of SAR 100 million converts into measurable operating capability, and where the ecosystem's revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد) are proven through contracts and execution.

The window is designed with three structural commitments: participation is **bounded** (it does not extend to the protected IP core or to open ecosystem ownership); capital is **staged** (released against milestones through governance gates, بوابات الحوكمة); and oversight is **real** (governance visibility, reporting rights, and Data Room access are built into the structure rather than promised around it).

The **reference post-money valuation of SAR 517 million (التقييم المرجعي بعد الاستثمار)** frames the scale of the wider ecosystem and is not a basis to recalculate the participation.

### Governance Table — الحوكمة

| Mechanism | Description |
|---|---|
| Decision gates (بوابات الحوكمة) | Each material capital release and field-spend phase passes a decision gate before proceeding. |
| Milestone-linked release | Capital deploys in tranches (40 / 60), each conditioned on documented milestones. |

### Decision Gates and Stakeholder Roles — البوابات والأدوار

| Stage | Gate function | Primary roles |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation (0–12 months) | Authorize base build and initial tranche. | Operating entity; founder; Partner (oversight). |
| Channel proof (12–36 months) | Authorize channel activation and second tranche. | Operating entity; founder; Partner (oversight). |
| Growth (Years 3–8) | Authorize scaling of proven channels. | Operating entity; governance; Partner (reporting). |
| Value maximization (Years 8–15) | Monitor long-term operating asset. | Governance; Partner (reporting). |

Stakeholder roles are defined so that **initiator, approver, and recorder are separated**, and so that the Partner's role is one of visibility and oversight rather than execution.

### Relationship to the Broader Ecosystem — العلاقة بالمنظومة الأوسع

The window is **contained within** the ecosystem but **bounded from** it. It draws on the ecosystem's layered capability — intelligence and exploration, governance and data, field operations, protected IP, and sustainability — while its participation and economics are assessed on the window's own terms. The protected IP core sits outside the window and is never transferred. The SAR 517 million reference describes ecosystem scale; the 30% describes the window.
