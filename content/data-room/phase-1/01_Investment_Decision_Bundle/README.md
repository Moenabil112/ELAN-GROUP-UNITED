### Purpose of the Package

This bundle prepares a senior decision-maker to make an informed judgment on a proposed strategic entry into the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** of ELAN Group United. It presents the decision, the entry logic, the window structure, the valuation framing, and a first-draft non-binding term sheet — in a form ready for professional review and, subsequently, conversion into Word or PDF. It is a decision package, not a marketing package. Every figure is illustrative and model-derived, and no figure constitutes a binding offer or a promise of return.

### Suggested Reading Order

1. **01 — Executive Investment Summary:** the decision in one reading.
2. **02 — Investor Entry Memo:** the logic behind the entry.
3. **03 — Local Operating Entry Window Structure:** what is being entered, and how it is governed.
4. **04 — Valuation Framing Memo:** the financial logic and the treatment of the reference valuation.
5. **05 — Non-Binding Term Sheet Outline:** the indicative terms for advisors to negotiate.

Read in sequence, the five documents move from decision, to rationale, to structure, to valuation, to indicative terms.

**Two framing rules held consistently across the package:**

1. The **30% participation** attaches to the **Local Operating Entry Window only**.
2. The **SAR 517 million** reference anchors ecosystem **scale**, and is **not** used to recalculate the participation.
