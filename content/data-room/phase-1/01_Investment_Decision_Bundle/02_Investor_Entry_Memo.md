### Document Purpose

This memo sets out the logic of the proposed strategic entry for reading by an investment committee. It explains why the entry is structured as a bounded participation in a defined operating window, how capital, governance, operations, and the financial model relate to one another, what the first 36 months are expected to build, and which matters require independent financial, legal, and operational review before any commitment. It is written as an institutional memo. All figures are illustrative and model-derived and none represents a binding offer or a promise of return.

---

### Executive Summary

The proposed entry is a **SAR 100 million (رأس مال الدخول)** strategic participation of **30% (الحصة التفاوضية)** in the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** of ELAN Group United. The window is a defined, investable unit within a larger sovereign-grade mining-intelligence ecosystem. The entry is priced against a **reference post-money valuation of SAR 517 million (التقييم المرجعي بعد الاستثمار)**, used as a scale anchor for the wider ecosystem and not as a basis to recalculate the participation. Capital is deployed on a staged, milestone-linked basis, and the Strategic Partner (الشريك الاستثماري) receives a defined economic participation together with governance visibility and reporting rights — without acquiring the protected IP core or operational control.

---

### 1. Purpose of the Investor Entry

The entry has a single, defined purpose: to fund the construction of a **local operating capability** and to give a Strategic Partner a bounded, governed participation in its economics and oversight. It is not a request for pass-through funding, and it is not an open equity stake in the whole ecosystem. It is a participation in one clearly delimited layer — the Local Operating Entry Window — with a defined percentage, a defined scope, and a defined review path to binding documentation.

### 2. Strategic Rationale for the SAR 100 Million Entry

Mining-intelligence capability is capital-intensive before it is revenue-generating. The strategic value of the SAR 100 million is that it converts capital into a **measurable, in-country operating capability** — team, governance, data and compute, field integration, sustainability function, and reserve — rather than into intangible promises. For the Strategic Partner, the rationale is exposure to a governed, staged build with transparent methodology and defined participation, inside a structure designed to protect capital through decision gates rather than to deploy it in a single, unrecoverable movement.

### 3. Definition of the Local Operating Entry Window

The **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** is the local operating layer of the ecosystem, ring-fenced and presented as its own investable unit. It is the layer where capital becomes operating capability and where the five revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد) are proven through contracts and execution. It is deliberately **distinct** from the protected IP core (the founder-owned intellectual property, held on a license-not-sale basis with clawback) and from the wider ecosystem valuation. The 30% participation attaches to this window exclusively.

### 4. Role of the Strategic Partner

The Strategic Partner enters as a **participating and governing party**, not as an operator and not as an acquirer of the IP core. The role carries:

- a defined **30% economic participation** in the window;
- **governance visibility** and defined **reporting rights (المراجعة المالية / التشغيلية inputs)**;
- **oversight of capital deployment** against milestones;
- **Data Room (غرفة البيانات والمستندات)** access to conduct independent review.

Operational control and the protected IP core remain undisturbed.

### 5. How the 30% Participation Is Positioned

The 30% is positioned as a **negotiated participation in the operating window**, priced against the reference post-money valuation as a scale anchor. It is **not** derived by applying a percentage to the SAR 517 million ecosystem reference, and the reference figure does not re-base the participation. This separation is deliberate: it keeps the participation attached to a defined operating unit whose economics can be reviewed on their own terms, and it keeps the ecosystem reference as context rather than as a pricing mechanism.

### 6. Relationship Between Capital, Governance, Operations, and the Financial Model

These four elements are designed to reinforce one another:

- **Capital** is released in tranches, each conditioned on documented milestones.
- **Governance** provides the decision gates (بوابات الحوكمة) that authorize each release and the reporting that evidences progress.
- **Operations** convert released capital into capability across the six deployment centers.
- **The financial model** (the live 15-year model) frames the horizon, the staging, and the sensitivity of outcomes to key assumptions.

No element operates in isolation: capital does not move without a gate, gates rely on operating evidence, and operating evidence is measured against the model.

### 7. What the Entry Enables During the First 36 Months

| Phase | Window | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 0–12 months | Establish the window, team, governance layer, and operating infrastructure. |
| Channel proof | 12–36 months | Convert capability into first contracts and operating indicators across the revenue channels. |

The first 36 months are structured to move the window from **build** to **proof** — establishing the operating base, then demonstrating that the capability converts into verifiable channels — before the longer growth and recovery phases of the 15-year horizon.

### 8. How the 15-Year Model Supports the Decision

The 15-year model supports the decision by making the horizon, the staging, and the sensitivities explicit rather than implied. It expresses outcomes as **modeled results under stated assumptions** — conservative, base, and expansion scenarios — not as promises. It allows the committee to see how value builds over the operating life of the window, how recovery is expected to develop across the horizon, and how sensitive the picture is to the primary assumptions. The model is a decision instrument; its figures are illustrative and subject to replacement with real data.

### 9. Relationship Between the Operating Window and the Broader Ecosystem

The Local Operating Entry Window sits within, but is bounded from, the broader ecosystem. The ecosystem is a layered, sovereign-grade operating system spanning intelligence and exploration, governance and data, field operations, protected IP, and sustainability. The window is the local operating layer of that system. The relationship is one of **containment with separation**: the window benefits from the ecosystem's capability and data loop, while its participation, economics, and valuation are assessed on the window's own terms. The SAR 517 million reference describes the ecosystem's scale; the 30% participation describes the window.

### 10. Key Matters Requiring Financial, Legal, and Operational Review

Before any commitment, the following require independent review:

- **Financial Review (المراجعة المالية):** validation of model assumptions, staging logic, channel economics, and the basis of the reference valuation.
- **Legal Review (المراجعة القانونية):** entity and participation structure, documentation of the 30% window interest, protection of the IP core (license-not-sale + clawback), governance and reserved-matter mechanics, and dispute mechanisms.
- **Operational Review (المراجعة التشغيلية):** the capability build plan, milestone definitions, governance-gate design, and reporting cadence.

All partner and channel assumptions in the model are **proposed**, not agreed; all figures are **illustrative** pending real data.

---

### Closing Note

This memo frames the entry logic to support an informed committee decision. The structure is deliberately bounded and governed: a defined participation, in a defined window, released against defined milestones, and conditional on independent review. It does not conclude the transaction; it prepares it for professional review and binding documentation.
