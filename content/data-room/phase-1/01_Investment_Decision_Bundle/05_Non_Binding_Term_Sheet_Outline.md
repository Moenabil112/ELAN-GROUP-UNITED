### Document Purpose

This is a **first-draft outline** for a non-binding term sheet (مذكرة شروط غير ملزمة). It organizes the indicative terms of the proposed entry into the standard sections so that advisors can develop them into a negotiated instrument. It is **not** a binding offer, does **not** create obligations, and is subject in every respect to independent financial, legal, and operational review and to definitive documentation. All figures are illustrative and model-derived.

> **Non-binding notice:** Nothing in this outline constitutes a commitment by any party. Terms are indicative placeholders for negotiation and are subject to professional review and binding documentation.

### 1. Transaction Overview — نظرة عامة على المعاملة

- **Parties:** [Strategic Partner — الشريك الاستثماري] and [ELAN Group United operating entity]. *(Parties placeholder — to be completed.)*
- **Purpose of the transaction:** A strategic entry by the Strategic Partner into the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** of ELAN Group United, to fund the construction of local operating capability in exchange for a defined participation and defined governance and reporting rights.
- **Nature:** Bounded participation in a defined operating window; **not** an acquisition of the protected IP core and **not** a transfer of operational control.

### 2. Entry Capital — رأس مال الدخول

- **Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول):** SAR 100 million *(illustrative)*.
- **Deployment approach:** Staged **40 / 60**, released against documented milestones through governance gates. No single-tranche disbursement.

### 3. Participation Scope — نطاق الحصة

- **Participation (الحصة التفاوضية):** 30%.
- **Scope:** The **Local Operating Entry Window exclusively (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)**.
- **Reference Post-money Valuation (التقييم المرجعي بعد الاستثمار):** SAR 517 million — volumetric reference for the wider ecosystem only; **not** a basis to recalculate the participation.
- **Exclusions:** No ownership of the protected IP core (license-not-sale + clawback); no transfer of operational control.

### 4. Governance and Reporting — الحوكمة والتقارير

- **Governance rights (placeholder):** Governance visibility into the window and its decision gates (بوابات الحوكمة); reserved-matter protections **[Professional Review]**.
- **Reporting rights (placeholder):** Periodic operating and financial reporting on a defined cadence *(cadence to be confirmed)*.
- **Accountability:** Human accountability retained; automation accelerates but never approves.

### 5. Capital Deployment — توظيف رأس المال

- **Use of Funds (توظيف رأس المال):** Deployed across six centers — Operations & Talent (34), Foundation & Governance (18), Local Data & Compute (16), Field Integration (14), Sustainability & Impact (8), Risk Reserve (10); total 100 *(illustrative, SAR m)*.
- **Oversight:** Strategic Partner oversight of deployment against milestones.
- **Release conditions:** Each tranche conditioned on documented milestones and gate approval.

### 6. Data Room and Review — غرفة البيانات والمراجعة

- **Data Room access (غرفة البيانات والمستندات):** Controlled access for the Strategic Partner to conduct review; access terms and any non-disclosure framework **[Professional Review]**.
- **Review scope:** Financial Review (المراجعة المالية), Legal Review (المراجعة القانونية), and Operational Review (المراجعة التشغيلية).

### 7. Conditions to Final Agreement — الشروط قبل الاتفاق النهائي

Entry into definitive documentation is conditional on, among other matters:

- satisfactory completion of financial, legal, and operational review;
- confirmation that the 30% participation is documented against the window, not the ecosystem reference;
- legal structuring of the window and the participation instrument **[Professional Review]**;
- confirmation of the IP-core boundary and operational-control retention;
- replacement of illustrative figures with confirmed data; and
- resolution of open items, including channel pricing **[To be defined]** and dispute mechanics **[Missing Input]**.
