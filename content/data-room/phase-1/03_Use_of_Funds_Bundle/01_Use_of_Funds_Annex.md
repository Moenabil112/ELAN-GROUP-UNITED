### Document Purpose

This annex is the master reference for the full SAR 100 million capital deployment (توظيف رأس المال). It sets out the deployment philosophy, the allocation across the six capital centers (مركز توظيف رأس المال), the operating capability (القدرة التشغيلية) each center creates, and how the whole deployment ties back to the 15-year financial model and the governance gates. The six per-center documents that follow expand each line. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; release is milestone-gated — evidence before spend.**

---

### Executive Summary

The SAR 100 million Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول) is not a general grant; it is converted, on a staged and gated basis, into a measurable local operating capability. The deployment is organized into six centers, released **40 / 60** against milestones, with **no single-tranche disbursement**. Each center has a defined purpose, an expected operating output, a governance checkpoint (نقطة تحقق حوكمية), and named review documents. Three cost lines — legal, compliance, and growth — are open (**[Missing Input]**) and flagged rather than assumed.

### Purpose of the SAR 100 Million Deployment

The deployment funds the construction of the Local Operating Entry Window's capability — the team, systems, field connection, governance base, impact function, and protective reserve that together let the window operate, report, and validate revenue. Its purpose is to turn capital into capability that a reviewer can measure, not into intangible promises.

### Capital Deployment Philosophy — فلسفة توظيف رأس المال

Four principles govern deployment: **evidence before spend** (each release is milestone-gated); **staging** (40 / 60, never a single tranche); **separation of duties** (recommender ≠ reviewer ≠ approver at every gate); and **reserve protection** (a dedicated reserve stands behind execution). Capital exposure is kept proportional to demonstrated progress throughout.

### Relationship to the 15-Year Financial Model

Each center maps to the model: Operations & Talent funds the central OPEX build-up (24→42 FTE × ~0.48 loaded); Data & Compute and Field Integration feed the CAPEX base (depreciated over 5 years); the reserve corresponds to the modeled risk-reserve coverage (~1.02× Y1). Capital staging (40 / 60) matches the model's deployment logic, and the deployment underpins the modeled revenue ramp (20→100% over Years 1–5) and Year-1 operating break-even.

### Relationship to Governance Gates

No center is released outside a gate. The primary capital-release gate is **G2 (financial)**; deployment decisions pass the 12-step decision flow (risk → financial → legal → recommendation → approval → logging → assignment → monitoring), and reserve draws are **ES-only**. Milestone releases are handled as T-F (milestone-gate) tasks and logged in the decision log.
