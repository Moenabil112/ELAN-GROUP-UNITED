### Document Purpose

This plan details the **SAR 34 million** allocation to Operations and Talent (العمليات والمواهب) — the capital center that funds the central operating team and, through it, the day-to-day capability of the window. It sets out the role categories, the hiring phases, the cost logic, and how staffing links to revenue activation and operational readiness. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; sub-allocations are working assumptions (WA) that sum to the center total.**

---

### Executive Summary

Operations and Talent is the largest capital center at **SAR 34 million (34%)** because people are the capability. The allocation funds the central OPEX build-up from roughly **24 to 42 FTE** across the first three years (at ~SAR 0.48m loaded per FTE), sequenced so the foundation team is in place first and expansion follows demonstrated readiness. Staffing is deliberately front-weighted toward the roles that stand up governance, finance, data, and operations, then broadened as channels are validated.

### Purpose of the Operations and Talent Allocation

The allocation builds and sustains the team that turns the ecosystem into a daily operating activity. It funds the central OPEX base in the model, and it is the capability that every other center depends on: without the team, systems, field assets, and governance instruments are inert.

### Relationship to Revenue Activation

The team is what converts capability into the model's revenue ramp (20→100% over Years 1–5). Channel-facing and delivery roles are sequenced so that the capacity to activate and service channels is in place ahead of the revenue it supports.

### Relationship to Operational Readiness

Operational readiness — the point at which the window can operate, report, and pass its gates — is gated on this center. Hiring is therefore milestone-linked (headcount gate → G2 financial), not open-ended, keeping payroll growth proportional to demonstrated readiness.
