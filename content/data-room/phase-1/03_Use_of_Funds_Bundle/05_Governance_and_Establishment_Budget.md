### Document Purpose

This budget details the **SAR 18 million** allocation to Establishment and Governance (التأسيس والحوكمة) — the capital center that constitutes the entity, stands up the governance structure, and installs the financial controls and approval workflows that protect every other dirham deployed. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; sub-allocations are working assumptions (WA) that sum to the center total; legal and compliance lines are flagged.**

### Executive Summary

Establishment and Governance at **SAR 18 million (18%)** builds the control fabric of the window: the entity, the governance structure (12 roles, 4 committees), the reporting framework, financial controls, and the approval workflows that enforce **separation of duties** (recommender ≠ reviewer ≠ approver). This center is why capital deployment is trustworthy — it installs the gates, the decision log, and the reporting cadence that stakeholders rely on. Legal and compliance sub-lines are carried here on an illustrative basis and flagged for definition.

### Capital Control Mechanisms — آليات ضبط رأس المال

Capital is controlled through milestone-gated release (evidence before spend), the G2 financial gate, 3-way match, separation of duties, ES-only reserve draws, and immutable decision-log entries for every release (T-F milestone-gate tasks).

### Relationship to Stakeholder Confidence

This center is the direct source of stakeholder confidence: the Strategic Partner's governance visibility, reporting rights, and capital-deployment oversight are all delivered by the structures funded here. Confidence rests not on assurances but on installed gates, controls, and an auditable decision log.
