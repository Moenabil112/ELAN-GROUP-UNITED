### Document Purpose

This budget details the **SAR 14 million** allocation to Field Integration (التكامل الميداني) — the layer that connects the window to the ground through field data capture, sensing, survey support, and site coordination. It sets out the components, workflows, evidence outputs, and governance checkpoints. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; sub-allocations are working assumptions (WA) that sum to the center total; field parameters are [Missing Input].**

---

### Executive Summary

Field Integration at **SAR 14 million (14%)** is the bridge between the ground and the dashboard. It funds field data capture, drone and survey support, sensors and measurement tools, field reporting workflows, and sample and site coordination — so that field activity produces governed, evidence-grade data that flows into the intelligence layers and the dashboard indicators. Field telemetry sits in the ecosystem's L3 layer; specific device parameters are **[Missing Input]** and enablement is gated at go-live (G9).

### Purpose of Field Integration

The center connects field operations to the governed data environment, turning site activity — surveys, sensing, samples, visits — into structured evidence. It is what makes field claims auditable and what feeds the field-derived indicators on the dashboard.

### Connection Between Field Activity and Dashboard Indicators

Field activity is only valuable to a reviewer if it produces governed data. This center ensures each field workflow yields structured, classified evidence that flows into the data environment (Doc 03) and surfaces as field-derived indicators on the dashboard — closing the loop between the ground and the model.

### Relationship to Mining Intelligence Layers

Field Integration feeds the ecosystem's layered intelligence: capture and sensing supply evidence to the intelligence/exploration layer (L1), governed through the data layer (L2), and reported to the impact layer (L5). It is the operational input to the mining-intelligence stack, not a standalone activity.
