### Document Purpose

This policy governs the **SAR 10 million** Risk Reserve (احتياطي المخاطر) — what it is for, when it may be used, who approves its use, what it covers, and how it is documented, monitored, and replenished. It is a control document. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; the reserve is held, not spent, and is drawn only under this policy.**

---

### Executive Summary

The Risk Reserve is a **SAR 10 million (10%)** buffer held to protect execution against delay, shortfalls, and short-term surprises. It is **held, not allocated to activity**, and drawn only on **ES approval** (recommended by FAL + RCL). In the model, it provides coverage of roughly **1.02× of the Y1 six-month OPEX exposure**. Its discipline is deliberate: reserve draws are the exception, are fully documented, are replenished before distributions, and are reported monthly.

### Purpose of the Risk Reserve

The reserve protects the deployment plan from being derailed by the ordinary hazards of execution — delays, cost overruns, and short-term shocks — without reopening the other capital centers. It keeps the plan resilient and the other allocations intact.

### Conditions for Reserve Use — شروط استخدام الاحتياطي

The reserve may be drawn only when: the need is outside the normal budget of a capital center; the event falls within a covered risk category; the draw is recommended by FAL + RCL; and ES approves. Routine budget variances are managed within centers, not from the reserve.

### Risk Reserve Use Case Table — حالات استخدام الاحتياطي

| Use case | Description |
|---|---|
| Execution delay | Bridging costs where documented milestones slip. |
| Cost overrun | Covering a validated overrun beyond a center's budget. |
| Short-term shock | Fuel/price/supply shocks affecting operations. |
| Scenario shift | Cushioning a shift from Base toward Conservative conditions. |
| Contingency | Genuine unforeseen events, fully documented. |

### Approval Authority Table — صلاحيات الاعتماد

| Step | Authority |
|---|---|
| Identification | Owner / functional lead flags the need. |
| Recommendation | FAL + RCL assess and recommend. |
| Approval | **ES only.** |
| Logging | Decision log entry (immutable). |
| Assignment | PMO assigns corrective task (T-E). |

### Documentation Requirement Table — متطلبات التوثيق

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Trigger evidence | Documented cause of the draw. |

| Recommendation | FAL + RCL sign-off recorded. |

### Relationship to Scenario Changes

The reserve is the operational cushion behind scenario shifts. A move from Base toward Conservative (slower revenue, higher cost) is precisely the condition the reserve is sized to absorb; a sustained shift triggers review of the deployment plan, not repeated reserve draws.

### Relationship to Delays or Cost Overruns

Delays and cost overruns are the primary covered categories. The reserve bridges validated, documented slippage — it does not fund scope expansion or convert into a general contingency for ordinary variance.

### Reserve Monitoring Table — مراقبة الاحتياطي

| Metric | Basis |
|---|---|
| Reserve balance | SAR 10m less cumulative approved draws. |
| Coverage ratio | ~1.02× of Y1 six-month OPEX exposure (model). |
| Draw log | Every draw logged with evidence. |
| Replenishment status | Plan to restore balance before distributions. |
| Reporting | Included in monthly financial (FAL) and risk (RCL) reports. |

### Replenishment or Adjustment Logic — منطق التجديد والتعديل

Drawn amounts are **replenished before any investor distribution** (reserve-first, per the distribution methodology). The reserve size may be adjusted only via a logged decision (FD-10), not by operating discretion.
