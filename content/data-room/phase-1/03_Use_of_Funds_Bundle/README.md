### Purpose of the Package

This bundle explains **how the SAR 100 million Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول) is transformed into measurable operating capability (القدرة التشغيلية)**. It details each of the six capital centers, the team and systems they build, and the milestone-based release plan and risk-reserve policy that keep deployment disciplined. It is prepared for readability by financial and operational reviewers and for conversion into Word or PDF. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; release is milestone-gated — evidence before spend.**

### Relationship to the 15-Year Financial Model

Each center maps to the model: Operations & Talent funds the central OPEX build-up (24→42 FTE × ~0.48 loaded); Local Data & Compute and Field Integration feed the CAPEX base (5-year depreciation); the Risk Reserve corresponds to the modeled coverage (~1.02× Y1). The release stages align to the model's milestones — establishment and Year-1 build, operating break-even (Year 1 base), the revenue ramp (20→100% over Years 1–5), and plateau growth (~6.2%/yr, Years 6–15).

### Relationship to Governance Gates

No center is released outside a gate. The primary capital-release gate is **G2 (financial)**; releases pass the 12-step decision flow (risk → financial → legal → recommendation → approval → logging → assignment → monitoring), are logged immutably as T-F milestone-gate tasks, and reserve draws are **ES-only**. Deployment respects the ecosystem's 12 approval gates (G1–G9 + OG-10/11/12) and separation of duties (recommender ≠ reviewer ≠ approver).
