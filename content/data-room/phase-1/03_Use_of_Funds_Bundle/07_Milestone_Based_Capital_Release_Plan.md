### Document Purpose

This plan sets out how the SAR 100 million is released over time — the stages, their objectives, the spending conditions, the governance approvals, and the evidence required before each release. It operationalizes the principle **evidence before spend** and ties capital release (صرف رأس المال) to the milestones of the 15-year model. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; release is milestone-gated.**

---

### Executive Summary

Capital is released in stages, not in a single tranche, on an overall **40 / 60** staging basis. Four stages sequence the deployment — **Foundation, Operating Readiness, Revenue Validation, and Expansion Readiness** — each with defined objectives, evidence requirements, and a governance approval (primary gate **G2 financial**). No stage opens until the prior stage's evidence is logged and approved, keeping capital exposure proportional to demonstrated progress and aligned to the model's milestone logic.

### Purpose of Phased Capital Release

Phased release protects capital by making each disbursement conditional on evidence. It converts the deployment plan into a sequence of gated decisions rather than a lump sum, so that spend follows demonstrated readiness and validated progress, and so that risk is contained at each step.

### Relationship to Financial Model Milestones

The stages map to the model: Foundation funds the establishment and OPEX base; Operating Readiness aligns to the Year-1 operating build and break-even; Revenue Validation aligns to the modeled revenue ramp (20→100% over Years 1–5); Expansion Readiness aligns to the plateau-growth phase (~6.2%/yr, Years 6–15).

### Relationship to Risk Control

Phased release is itself the primary capital risk control: staged exposure, evidence-gated disbursement, separation of duties, and an immutable log. The Risk Reserve (Doc 06) stands behind the plan for events the stages cannot absorb.

### Reporting Requirements

Each release and its evidence are recorded in the decision log and reflected in the weekly execution report (PMO) and the monthly financial (FAL) and risk (RCL) reports; stage transitions are reported to ES at the quarterly executive review.
