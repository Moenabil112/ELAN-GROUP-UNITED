### Purpose of the Package

This bundle explains **how decisions, capital deployment, risks, reporting, and stakeholder oversight are governed** in the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية). It sets out the risk register, the governance gates, the reporting cadence, the decision-rights matrix, the capital-control and data-governance policies, and the independent-review protocol. It is prepared for readability by governance, risk, and investment reviewers and for conversion into Word or PDF. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; this bundle sets governance process, not legal or financial conclusions.**

### Relationship to Capital Deployment

Governance is inseparable from the SAR 100m deployment. Capital is released **40 / 60** through governance gates (primary gate **G2 financial**), under separation of duties (initiator ≠ approver ≠ recorder), a 3-way match, an ES-only reserve, and immutable logging. Freezes and reallocations are ES-approved and logged. The Governance Gates Framework and Capital Control Policy are the two faces of this discipline.

### Relationship to the 15-Year Financial Model

Gates map to model milestones (establishment, Year-1 build and operating break-even, the revenue ramp, plateau growth). Reporting ties to the model (revenue vs. ramp; scenario position across Conservative / Base / Expansion). Independent financial review validates the model's assumptions, staging, and reference-valuation basis before any commitment.

### Relationship to Stakeholder Oversight

Stakeholder oversight — including the Strategic Partner's governance visibility and reporting rights — is delivered by these instruments: RBAC-governed reporting on a fixed cadence, an auditable decision log, a data room governed by classification and access tags, and an independent-review protocol. Oversight rests on installed controls, not assurances.
