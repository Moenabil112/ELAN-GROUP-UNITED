### Document Purpose

This framework defines the governance gates (بوابات الحوكمة) that authorize each phase of capital deployment and operation in the Local Operating Entry Window. It sets out the gate sequence, the evidence and approvals required to pass each, the link to capital release and the 15-year model, and the review standards applied. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; no capital moves outside a gate.**

---

### Executive Summary

Capital and operational progress advance through seven window gates, each mapped to the ecosystem's underlying approval gates (G1–G9 + OG-10/11/12). A gate is a decision checkpoint: nothing passes without the required evidence, the required approval, and an immutable log entry. The gates are the mechanism by which the **40 / 60 staged capital release** is tied to demonstrated progress and to the model's milestones — from foundation, through operating and data readiness and first revenue validation, to financial review and expansion readiness.

---

### Governance Reference

12 approval gates (G1 strategic · G2 financial · G3 legal · G4 contracts · G5 external comms · G6 partner · G7 investor docs · G8 regulatory · G9 go-live · OG-10 high-risk AI · OG-11 sensitive data · OG-12 workflow change). Primary capital-release gate: **G2 (financial)**. Decision flow: 12 steps; separation of duties throughout. Capital: SAR 100m, staged 40 / 60.

---

### Purpose of Governance Gates

Gates exist to make progress conditional on evidence. They convert the deployment and operating plan into a sequence of controlled decisions, so that authority, spend, and go-live are granted only when the prior stage is demonstrably complete — keeping capital exposure proportional to progress and every decision auditable.

### Review Standards — معايير المراجعة

Each gate applies the same standards: evidence must be documented and sufficient; the decision must pass the 12-step flow (risk → financial → legal → recommendation → approval → logging → assignment → monitoring); the approval must sit with the authorized role; and the outcome must be logged immutably as a T-F (milestone-gate) task. Reserve draws are handled separately and are ES-only.
