### Document Purpose

This document defines the decision rights (حقوق القرار) for the Local Operating Entry Window — who recommends, reviews, and approves each category of decision, at what approval level, and how decisions escalate. It operationalizes the principle that **recommender ≠ reviewer ≠ approver**. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; role holders are [Missing Input] pending appointment.**

---

### Executive Summary

Decision rights are assigned by category — strategic, financial, capital deployment, hiring, commercial, data access, and risk escalation — each with a defined recommender, reviewer, and approver, and a tier (T0–T3) that fixes how much authority may ever be delegated. The highest-sensitivity matters (cap table, IP terms, legal commitments, partner terms, public claims) are **T0 human-only** and pass through the founder/ES apex. Every material decision runs the 12-step flow and is logged immutably; **AI never approves**.

### Governance Reference

12 roles (ES apex · EA · AGL · DAL · TSA · ADM · FAL · LCL · OAL · RCL · PMO · DKL); 4 committees; 15-category Authority Matrix; RACI (14 rows); tiers T0–T3. Separation of duties is absolute.

---

### Purpose of Decision Rights

Decision rights exist to ensure that authority is explicit, separated, and matched to the sensitivity of the decision. They prevent any single actor from initiating and approving the same action, and they make every decision traceable to a named approver.

### Decision Categories — فئات القرار

Strategic · Financial · Capital deployment · Hiring · Commercial · Data access · Risk escalation.

### Decision Rights Matrix — مصفوفة حقوق القرار

| Decision category | Recommender | Reviewer | Approver | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic decisions | EA | ES committee | ES / Founder | T0 |

| Capital deployment | FAL | RCL | G2 / ES | T1/T0 |
| Hiring decisions | ADM | FAL (budget) | ES / OAL (per level) | T2 |
| Commercial decisions | OAL | LCL (contract) | G4 / ES | T1 |
| Data access decisions | DAL | TSA | AGL / ES (sensitive) | T1/T0 |
| Risk escalation | RCL | — (independent) | ES | T0/T1 |
| IP / partner / public claims | LCL / ES | ES committee | ES / Founder | T0 |

*T0 = human-only (no delegation); T1 = AI-assisted, human decides; T2 = AI drafts, human approves; T3 = autonomous monitoring only.*

### Escalation Table — جدول التصعيد

| Trigger | Escalation path |
|---|---|
| Decision outside mandate | Functional lead → ES |
| Risk threshold breach | RCL → ES (S1 ≤ 24h) |
| Commitment attempted outside a gate | RCL → ES (blocked) |
