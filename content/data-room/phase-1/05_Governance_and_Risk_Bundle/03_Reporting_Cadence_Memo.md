### Document Purpose

This memo defines the reporting cadence (دورية التقارير) for the Local Operating Entry Window — the rhythm of monthly, quarterly, and annual reporting across financial, operational, risk, and governance reporting, plus data-room updates, scenario updates, and stakeholder review meetings. It sets who reports what, to whom, how often, and how issues escalate. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

Reporting follows a fixed rhythm so that stakeholders — including the Strategic Partner — receive timely, consistent visibility. The core cadence is **weekly execution** (PMO), **monthly** governance/risk/financial/AI reports (ADM/RCL/FAL/AGL), and a **quarterly executive review** (ES), on a continuous decision log, with event-driven exception reports. Financial reporting ties to the 15-year model; scenario updates flag shifts between Conservative, Base, and Expansion; and escalation is defined so that S1 issues reach ES within 24 hours.

---

### Governance Reference

Reporting owned across the 12 roles; RCL is the independent risk line; ES chairs the quarterly review. Data-room updates follow the deposit convention (ELAN-{DOMAIN}-{TYPE}-v{n}). Recipients are governed by RBAC (founder, board, investor, lender, legal).

---

### Purpose of Reporting

Reporting exists to give stakeholders governed, timely visibility into performance, risk, and capital — and to create the audit trail that makes oversight real. It replaces ad hoc updates with a predictable cadence and a clear ownership and escalation path.
