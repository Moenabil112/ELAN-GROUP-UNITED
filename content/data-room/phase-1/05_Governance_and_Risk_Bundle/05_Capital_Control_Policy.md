### Document Purpose

This policy governs control (ضبط رأس المال) over the SAR 100 million deployment — how capital is released, budgeted, thresholded, procured, documented, and reported, and the conditions for reserve use, capital freeze (تجميد الصرف), and capital reallocation (إعادة توجيه رأس المال). It is a control document tied directly to the governance gates. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; thresholds are [Missing Input] pending FD-09/FD-10.**

---

### Executive Summary

Capital control rests on a small set of hard rules: **staged 40 / 60 release** through governance gates; **separation of duties** (initiator ≠ approver ≠ recorder); **3-way match** on procurement; a **weekly cash view** and **monthly financial update**; and an **ES-only reserve**. Spend follows budget and thresholds, is documented and variance-reported, and can be **frozen** or **reallocated** only under defined conditions and approvals. Payment thresholds and reserve policy detail are open items (FD-09/FD-10) and are flagged rather than assumed.

---

### Governance Reference

Owned by FAL under the Investment & Finance committee, with RCL independent oversight. Primary release gate: **G2 (financial)**. Reserve: **ES-only**. Capital: SAR 100m across six centers, staged 40 / 60. 13 financial controls apply.

---

### Purpose of Capital Control

Capital control ensures that every dirham is released against evidence, spent within budget and authority, documented, and reviewable — and that deviations are caught and corrected. It is what makes the deployment trustworthy to stakeholders and resilient to error or misuse.

### Budget Approval, Procurement, and Documentation

- **Budget approval:** each center's budget is approved before release; changes require committee approval.
- **Procurement review:** purchases pass procurement review with 3-way match; ABC/COI policies apply.
- **Expense documentation:** every expense is documented and evidenced; unsupported spend is blocked.

### Variance Reporting Table — تقارير الانحراف

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Variance measure | Actual vs. approved per center. |
| Reporting | Monthly financial report (FAL). |
| Threshold breach | Alert (budget variance); escalate to ES. |
| Corrective action | T-E corrective task; re-approve at owning gate. |

### Reserve Usage

Reserve use follows the Risk Reserve Policy (Use of Funds Bundle): covered categories only, FAL + RCL recommendation, **ES-only** approval, full documentation, and **replenishment before distributions**.

### Capital Freeze Conditions — شروط تجميد الصرف

Capital may be **frozen (تجميد الصرف)** on: a material unresolved risk (S1), a governance breach (commitment outside a gate), a sustained adverse scenario shift, or a failed gate. A freeze is ES-approved and logged; release resumes only when the trigger is cleared and re-approved.

### Capital Reallocation Table — إعادة توجيه رأس المال

| Condition | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Validated need to move funds between centers. |
| Recommendation | FAL (+ RCL where risk-related). |
| Approval | ES (logged). |
| Limits | Within the SAR 100m envelope; open categories (legal/compliance/growth) **[Missing Input]**. |
| Evidence | Documented rationale; decision-log entry. |

### Relationship to Governance Gates

Every release is a gate decision (primary G2), and freezes, reallocations, and reserve draws are all gated and logged. Capital control and the Governance Gates Framework (Document 02) are two views of the same discipline: no capital moves without evidence, authority, and a log entry.
