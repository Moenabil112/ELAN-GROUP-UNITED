### Document Purpose

This is the master risk register (سجل المخاطر) for the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية). It records the main risk categories, their level, trigger, financial and operational impact, governance response (الاستجابة الحوكمية), review owner, review frequency, and required evidence. It is a living control document, reviewed on a fixed cadence. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; risk levels are working assessments, not statements of fact.**

---

### Executive Summary

The window's risks cluster into eight categories, from revenue-validation and mining-execution risk to legal, governance, and capital-deployment risk. Each is matched to a specific governance response rather than a general assurance: staged, gate-conditioned capital release; an independent-review requirement before commitment; a controlled data room; and a dedicated risk reserve. The two structural risks to watch are **buyer concentration** (the sovereign operating channel is the largest NPV) and **counsel not yet engaged** (which gates every legal item). Risk ownership sits with named roles, with RCL (Risk & Audit) as the independent line.

---

### Governance Reference

Governed under the ecosystem's control architecture: 12 executive roles, 4 committees (Executive Steering, AI Governance, Investment & Finance, Risk & Audit), 12 approval gates (G1–G9 + OG-10/11/12), a 12-step decision flow, and separation of duties (recommender ≠ reviewer ≠ approver). Capital: SAR 100m, staged 40 / 60. Horizon: 15-year model.

### Required Evidence — الأدلة المطلوبة

Each risk entry is supported by evidence: channel-validation records (revenue), field results and independent review (execution), data-quality documentation, adoption reports, decision-log entries (governance), counsel opinions (legal), distribution records (payback), and capital-release evidence (deployment).
