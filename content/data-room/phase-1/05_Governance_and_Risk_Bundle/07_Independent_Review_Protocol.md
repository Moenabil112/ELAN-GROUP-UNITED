### Document Purpose

This protocol defines the independent review (المراجعة المستقلة) that must precede any final commitment — its financial, legal, operational, technical, and data scopes, the reviewer requirements, timing, deliverables, and how management responds. It is the mechanism that converts the bundles from an internal position into an externally validated basis for decision. **This document is not legal, financial, or tax advice; it sets the review process, not its conclusions.**

---

### Executive Summary

Independent review spans five domains — financial, legal, operational, technical, and data — each with a defined scope, a required reviewer, and deliverables that feed the final decision. Legal review depends on appointing licensed counsel (LD-14, the top blocker), and every contract instrument is **[Licensed Review]**. Reviews are sequenced so that findings inform one another, management responds to each finding, and the outcome supports — but does not replace — the decision-maker's judgment. No figure or structure is treated as confirmed until the relevant review closes.

---

### Governance Reference

Coordinated by ES with functional owners (FAL, LCL, OAL, TSA, DAL); RCL independent oversight. Every conclusion in a review is filed in the data room. Contract instruments and legal conclusions are **[Licensed Review]**; counsel appointment (LD-14) gates legal review.

---

### Purpose of Independent Review

Independent review exists so that the decision rests on validated evidence, not internal assertion. It tests the financial model, the legal structure, operational readiness, the technical build, and data integrity against independent standards, and surfaces findings the decision-maker must weigh before committing.

### Data Review Scope and Final Decision Support

- **Data review scope:** integrity of the data room, classification, access control, audit trail, and the control of model assumptions and financial data (Document 06).
- **Final decision support:** consolidated findings and management responses are assembled to support the decision-maker; the review informs the decision but does not replace human judgment or the governance gates.

### Reviewer Requirements

Reviewers must be independent of management, qualified in their domain, and (for legal) licensed in the relevant jurisdictions. Conclusions are filed in the data room; no review conclusion is treated as adopted until the accountable human adopts it.
