### Document Purpose

This schedule details how the SAR 100 million Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول) is deployed across the six approved capital centers — the purpose of each, its expected output, spending category, timing, governance checkpoint, required review documents, and link to the financial model. It is the operational counterpart to the Use of Funds (توظيف رأس المال) summary in the model. **All allocations are illustrative and model-derived; release is milestone-gated — evidence before spend.**

---

### Executive Summary

Capital is deployed across six centers totalling **SAR 100 million**, released on a **staged 40 / 60 basis** against milestones, with **no single-tranche disbursement**. Each center maps to defined spending categories, an owner, and a governance gate. Three categories — **legal, compliance, and growth** — are **[Missing Input]** pending definition and are flagged rather than assumed.

### 1. Operations and Talent — العمليات والمواهب · SAR 34m

- **Purpose:** Build and sustain the team that runs the window day to day; funds the central OPEX base.
- **Expected output:** Operating team (24→42 FTE build), active operations, day-to-day capability.
- **Spending category:** Human resources (ramped Y1–Y3); operations (recurring).
- **Timing:** Ramped Y1–Y3.
- **Governance checkpoint:** Headcount gate (ADM + FAL); PMO gates (OAL).
- **Review documents required:** Headcount plan; org and roles; OPEX build-up.
- **Financial model link:** Assumptions r.30; OPEX sheet.

### 2. Establishment and Governance — التأسيس والحوكمة · SAR 18m

- **Purpose:** Establish the systemic, legal, and governance base of the window.
- **Expected output:** Constituted entity, governance instruments, document base.
- **Spending category:** Setup (Y1); documentation (recurring).
- **Timing:** Front-loaded Y1, then recurring.
- **Governance checkpoint:** G2 tranche 1 (LCL + ADM); ADM (DKL).
- **Review documents required:** Establishment file; governance charter; documents register.
- **Financial model link:** Assumptions r.29; Use of Funds sheet.

### 3. Local Data and Computing — البيانات والحوسبة المحلية · SAR 16m

- **Purpose:** Build in-country data memory and analysis / compute capability (data sovereignty).
- **Expected output:** Local data base, compute environment, analysis capability.
- **Spending category:** Technology (Y1); data (Y1–Y2).
- **Timing:** Y1–Y2.
- **Governance checkpoint:** Phase gates (TSA / DAL).
- **Review documents required:** Technical spec; data governance plan; residency design.
- **Financial model link:** Assumptions r.31; CAPEX sheet.

### 4. Field Integration — التكامل الميداني · SAR 14m

- **Purpose:** Connect the window to the field — teams, sensing, and site infrastructure.
- **Expected output:** Field-integration layer; site connectivity; asset base.
- **Spending category:** Infrastructure (Y2).
- **Timing:** Y2 (asset build).
- **Governance checkpoint:** Asset gate (OAL).
- **Review documents required:** Field-integration plan; asset register; CAPEX schedule.
- **Financial model link:** Assumptions r.32; CAPEX sheet.

### 5. Sustainability and Impact — الاستدامة والأثر · SAR 8m

- **Purpose:** Fund the measured-impact (L5) function and responsible-finance linkage.
- **Expected output:** Impact measurement; KPI framework (design targets); responsible-finance readiness.
- **Spending category:** Sustainability (L5).
- **Timing:** Phased across the horizon.
- **Governance checkpoint:** L5 / sustainability review.
- **Review documents required:** Sustainability KPI framework; impact methodology.
- **Financial model link:** Assumptions r.33.

### 6. Risk Reserve — احتياطي المخاطر · SAR 10m

- **Purpose:** Protect execution against delay, shortfalls, and short-term surprises.
- **Expected output:** Funded reserve; coverage ~1.02× of 6-month OPEX exposure (Y1).
- **Spending category:** Risk reserve (held); contingency (held).
- **Timing:** Held; drawn only on approval.
- **Governance checkpoint:** ES only (FAL + RCL).
- **Review documents required:** Reserve policy (FD-10); draw-down rules.
- **Financial model link:** Assumptions r.34; Risk Reserve sheet.
