### Document Purpose

This pack shows how the 15-Year Financial Model responds to changes in its key drivers — which levers move outcomes most, and how management would respond. It supports financial review by making the model's sensitivities explicit. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; sensitivities are modeled responses, not forecasts.**

---

### Executive Summary

Sensitivity Analysis (تحليل الحساسية) confirms that two levers dominate: the **discount rate** and the **revenue factor**. Value moves ≈ **−SAR 35m per +1%** discount-rate increase (11→12%), and ≈ **±SAR 50m per ±0.1** in the revenue factor. Timing, cost, distribution policy, and reserve use matter, but through their effect on the same underlying cash flows. The discount rate is the **declared negotiation variable**, which is why methodology transparency is treated as a first-order commitment.

### Review Points

- **Discount rate** and **revenue** are the dominant levers; everything else acts through cash flow.
- **Founder value** figures are cap-table outputs, not window-partner returns.
- Timing and reserve effects are largely **directional** pending real data.

---

### Closing Note

Sensitivity analysis focuses attention where it belongs: on the discount rate, revenue growth, and the speed of contract validation. Managing these — transparently and through staged, gated release — is the core of protecting value across the 15-year horizon. All sensitivities remain illustrative pending real data.
