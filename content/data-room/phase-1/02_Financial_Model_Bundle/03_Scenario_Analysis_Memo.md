### Document Purpose

This memo explains the three scenarios the 15-Year Financial Model runs — Conservative (السيناريو المحافظ), Base (السيناريو الأساسي), and Expansion (سيناريو التوسع) — and how each behaves across revenue, cost, distributions, payback, risk, and validation. It is written to help an investment committee read outcomes as a **range under stated levers**, not as a single number. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; scenarios are modeled results, not promises.**

---

### Executive Summary

Scenario Analysis (تحليل السيناريوهات) is driven by three levers: the discount rate, a revenue factor, and a central-OPEX factor. Moving from Base to Conservative lowers revenue and raises both discount and cost; moving to Expansion does the reverse. Under Base, the model computes an ecosystem value of **SAR 901m** and operating break-even in **Year 1**; Conservative gives **SAR 727m** and break-even in **Year 2**; Expansion gives **SAR 1,076m** and break-even in **Year 1**. The discount rate is the **declared negotiation variable** and the single most influential lever.

### 1. Conservative Scenario — السيناريو المحافظ

- **Definition:** Cautious case — revenue 15% below base, discount 12.5%, OPEX 5% above base.
- **Revenue growth logic:** Slower channel activation; plateau reached later; ramp discounted by 0.85.
- **Cost behavior:** Central OPEX runs ~5% higher, compressing early-year margins.
- **Distribution behavior:** Distributions begin later and are thinner; reserve-first applied strictly.
- **Payback reading:** Investor capital payback pushed **beyond the base-case path** (later than ~Year 8); modeled, not guaranteed.
- **Risk level:** Lower downside surprise; the scenario is itself the stress buffer.
- **Validation requirements:** Confirm channels can still activate under slower assumptions; confirm reserve adequacy.
- **Implications for the Strategic Partner (الشريك الاستثماري):** Longer horizon to distributions; strongest test of reserve discipline and staging.

### 2. Base Scenario — السيناريو الأساسي

- **Definition:** Central case at the declared 11% red-line discount, revenue and OPEX at 1.00.
- **Revenue growth logic:** Ramp 20→100% over Years 1–5; +6.2%/yr thereafter.
- **Cost behavior:** Central OPEX as modeled; blended margin ~46%.
- **Distribution behavior:** Distributions from window cash flow after reserves and governance approval.
- **Payback reading:** Preliminary base reading studies a payback path **around Year 8**, conditional on channel conversion and an adopted distribution policy.
- **Risk level:** Balanced — the reference against which the other two are read.
- **Validation requirements:** Confirm channel pricing **[To be defined]** and margin assumptions.
- **Implications for the Strategic Partner:** The reference case for decision discussion and term negotiation.

### 3. Expansion Scenario — سيناريو التوسع

- **Definition:** Upside case — revenue 15% above base, discount 10%, OPEX 10% above base (to support scale).
- **Revenue growth logic:** Faster channel activation and higher plateau throughput.
- **Cost behavior:** Higher OPEX to fund scale, offset by stronger revenue.
- **Distribution behavior:** Earlier and larger distributions, subject to reserve and governance.
- **Payback reading:** Investor capital payback path **earlier than the base** (before ~Year 8); modeled, not guaranteed.
- **Risk level:** Higher execution demand; upside is conditional on readiness keeping pace with spend.
- **Validation requirements:** Confirm operational capacity to absorb faster scaling without capital waste.
- **Implications for the Strategic Partner:** Best return profile, but most dependent on execution discipline.

### Review Points

- Scenarios are **modeled ranges**, not forecasts to be relied upon.
- **Founder-attributed value** figures are cap-table outputs, distinct from **window-partner distributions**.
- Payback readings are **directional and conditional**, never guaranteed.

---

### Closing Note

The value of scenario analysis is that it makes the range and its drivers explicit. Base sets the reference, Conservative sets the discipline, Expansion sizes the upside — and the discount rate ties them together as the declared negotiation variable. All three remain illustrative pending real data.
