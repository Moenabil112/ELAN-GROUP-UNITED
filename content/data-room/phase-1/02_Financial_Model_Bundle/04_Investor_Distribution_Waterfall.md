### Document Purpose

This document explains **how** the Strategic Partner's distributions (توزيعات الشريك الاستثماري) are calculated — the source, the sequence, the reserve and governance conditions, and how distributions relate to payback. It sets out a **methodology**, because window-level distribution figures are not yet fixed. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived. There is no guaranteed return and no fixed distribution date; nothing here is an offer or a commitment.**

---

### Executive Summary

Distributions to the 30% participant flow from **window operating cash flow, after reserves are funded and after governance approval** — not from a fixed coupon. The sequence is a simple, disciplined waterfall: operating cash → reserve funding → governance approval → distributable cash → 30% participation share. Distribution timing and size vary by scenario and are conditional on channels converting to actual revenue. Specific window-level distribution amounts are **[To be defined]** pending model confirmation and an adopted distribution policy.

### 1. Distribution Overview — نظرة عامة على التوزيعات

Distributions are the mechanism by which the Strategic Partner participates in the economics of the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية). They are **residual and conditional**: they arise only after the window generates operating cash, funds its reserves, and receives governance approval to distribute. They are not interest, not a dividend guarantee, and not tied to a fixed calendar.

### 2. Participation Basis — أساس الحصة

The participation basis is **30% of the Local Operating Entry Window**. The 30% applies to **distributable window cash flow**, not to gross revenue and not to the ecosystem valuation. It is not derived from, and does not change with, the SAR 517m reference.

### 3. Cash Flow to Distribution Logic — من التدفق النقدي إلى التوزيع

| Step | Description |
|---|---|
| 1. Operating cash flow | Window generates operating cash from validated channels. |
| 2. Reserve funding | Risk reserve and required buffers funded first (coverage ~1.02× Y1 basis). |
| 3. Governance approval | Distribution proposed, reviewed, and approved through governance gates (بوابات الحوكمة). |
| 4. Distributable cash | Remaining cash after reserves and approved commitments. |
| 5. Participation share | Strategic Partner receives 30% of distributable cash. |

### 4. Distribution Waterfall — شلال التوزيع

```
Window operating cash flow
        │
        ▼
   Fund reserves & required buffers        (reserve-first)
        │
        ▼
   Governance approval to distribute        (بوابات الحوكمة)
        │
        ▼
   Distributable cash
        │
        ├── 30% → Strategic Partner (الشريك الاستثماري)
        └── 70% → Window / other participants
```

*Waterfall is indicative; exact tiers, priorities, and any preferences are **[Professional Review]** items for definitive documentation.*
