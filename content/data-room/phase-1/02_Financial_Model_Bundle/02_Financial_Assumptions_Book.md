### Document Purpose

This book documents every material assumption behind the 15-Year Financial Model (النموذج المالي لمدة 15 سنة) so that a reviewer can test the model rather than take its outputs on trust. Each assumption is labelled as an input or a working assumption (WA), and open items are marked. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; assumptions are never presented as facts, and none constitutes an offer or a promise of return.**

---

### Executive Summary

The model rests on a small set of transparent levers: a **11% base discount rate** (declared red line), a five-year revenue ramp to plateau with ~6.2% annual growth thereafter, channel-specific net margins averaging ~46% blended, a central OPEX build-up, a 5-year depreciation life, and a 75/45-day working-capital cycle. Capital is SAR 100 million across six centers, staged 40/60. Scenario behavior is driven by three levers (discount rate, revenue factor, OPEX factor). The most important discipline in this book: **every assumption is a working assumption pending real data**, and the model is built to be re-run as real data replaces illustrative inputs.
