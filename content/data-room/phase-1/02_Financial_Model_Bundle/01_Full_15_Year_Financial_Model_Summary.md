### Document Purpose

This document is the structured entry point to the 15-Year Financial Model (النموذج المالي لمدة 15 سنة). It summarizes what the model does, its main outputs, and the logic behind revenue, cost, cash flow, distributions, and payback — so a financial reviewer or investment committee can read the dashboard numbers with confidence. It is a summary of a live model (ELAN-FIN-MODEL-v0.2, 16 sheets), not a valuation opinion. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived, are working assumptions pending real data, and are not an offer or a promise of return.**

---

### Executive Summary

The 15-year model translates the ecosystem's five revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد), its cost base, and its capital deployment into a coherent set of outputs at an **11% base discount rate** — the declared negotiation red line. On a base case, the model computes a total channel NPV of **SAR 736 million**, a model ecosystem value of **SAR 901 million** (channels + IP-Co center), and **SAR 4,090 million** in cumulative 15-year revenue, with operating break-even reached in **Year 1**.

These are the **model's internal outputs**. The **investment framing** sits on top of them: a **SAR 100 million Entry Capital (رأس مال الدخول)**, a **30% Participation (الحصة التفاوضية)** in the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)**, priced against a **Reference Post-money Valuation (التقييم المرجعي بعد الاستثمار) of SAR 517 million** used as a volumetric anchor only. The reference is deliberately conservative relative to the model's computed ecosystem value, and neither figure is used to recalculate the 30% participation.

### Revenue Logic — منطق الإيراد

Revenue is built from **five channels (مسارات الإيراد)**, each with its own net margin and each treated as **verifiable, not contracted**: sovereign operating and governance fees (55% margin), programmatic exploration/analysis (40%), data and remote sensing (45%), field hardware and edge integration (30%), and field services and reporting (35%). Channels ramp from 20% to 100% of plateau across Years 1–5 and grow ~6.2%/year thereafter. Contracted pricing per channel is **[To be defined]**; no signed contract is claimed.

### Cost Logic — منطق التكلفة

Cost is driven by a central OPEX build-up (headcount at ~SAR 0.48m loaded per FTE, growing 3%/yr after Year 5; G&A growing 5%/yr) plus CAPEX depreciated over 5 years. Blended channel contribution margin holds around **46%** at plateau. Unallocated categories (legal, compliance, advisory) are **[Missing Input]** pending definition.

### Cash Flow Logic — منطق التدفق النقدي

Cash flow combines channel net contribution, central OPEX, CAPEX, and a working-capital cycle of ~30 days. Capital is released in tranches (40 / 60) against milestones, keeping cash exposure proportional to progress. Year-1 monthly burn is ~SAR 1.63m with end-Y1 runway of ~4.7 months on the modeled staging.

### Investor Distribution Logic — منطق توزيعات الشريك

Investor Distributions (توزيعات الشريك الاستثماري) to the 30% participant derive from **window operating cash flow after reserves and governance approval**, not from a fixed coupon. The distribution methodology is set out in Document 04; specific distribution figures at window level are **[To be defined]** and there is **no guaranteed return and no fixed distribution date**.

### Payback Reading — قراءة استرداد رأس المال

**Payback (استرداد رأس المال)** for the SAR 100m entry is assessed on a **cumulative-distribution basis** and is distinct from the model's **operating break-even (Year 1 base)**. On a preliminary base-case reading, a long-term payback path can be *studied around Year 8* **if** channels convert to actual contracts and a distribution policy is adopted. This is a modeled path, not a guarantee. Full methodology is in Document 05.
