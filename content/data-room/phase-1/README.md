### Purpose of the Phase 1 Data Room

This is the complete **Phase 1** document package for the ELAN Group United Stakeholder Data Room. It organizes, in one coherent structure, everything a senior decision-maker and their advisors need to review the proposed strategic entry into the **Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية)** — from the investment decision and the 15-year financial model, through use of funds, revenue channels, governance and risk, ecosystem and operations, and the legal review path, to the document-control layer that governs the room itself.

The package is prepared for **controlled review** and for later conversion into Word or PDF. It supports an informed decision; it does not conclude one. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived and subject to independent professional review; nothing here is an offer, a commitment, or a promise of return.**

### Suggested Reading Order

**For a first-look decision-maker:**
1. **01 — Investment Decision** (start with the Executive Investment Summary).
2. **02 — Financial Model** (the 15-year dashboard).
3. **05 — Governance & Risk** and **06 — Ecosystem & Operations** (how it is governed and run).
4. **07 — Legal & Regulatory Review** (the review path).

**For a full review:** 01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 08, with **08 — Data Room & Document Control** used throughout for navigation (its `Data Room Index` maps every document).

**For advisors:** financial advisors start at 02/03; commercial at 04; governance/risk at 05; operational/technical at 06; legal counsel at 07; the administrator at 08.
