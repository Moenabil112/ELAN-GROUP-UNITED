### Document Purpose

This roadmap sets out how the revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد) are activated during the first 36 months — the commercial objective, the sequencing of channels, the phase priorities, the operating readiness and capacity required, the sales and contracting workflow, and the link to the 15-year model. **All channels are verifiable, not contracted; all figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

The commercial objective for the first 36 months is to move the window from **positioning to validated, contracted revenue** across a sequenced set of channels — proving the recurring and early-income channels first, then the program and data channels, then the capability-dependent integration channel. The roadmap runs in four phases (Foundation & Positioning, First Revenue Validation, Contract Conversion, Commercial Expansion Readiness), aligned to the model's revenue ramp (20 → 60% of plateau across Years 1–3).

### Commercial Objective for the First 36 Months

To validate and begin converting the revenue channels into contracted, deliverable revenue — establishing the recurring operating base and early field income first, proving program and data demand next, and preparing the integration channel — while building the sales, proposal, and contracting discipline that supports expansion beyond Month 36.

### 36-Month Roadmap Table — خارطة الطريق (36 شهرًا)

| Phase | العربية | Window | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation & Positioning | مرحلة التأسيس والتموضع | 0–12 months | Stand up commercial capability; position channels; first proposals. |
| 2. First Revenue Validation | مرحلة التحقق الأول من الإيراد | 6–18 months | Validate Wave 1 channels; first contracts. |
| 3. Contract Conversion | مرحلة التحويل التعاقدي | 12–30 months | Convert Wave 2 opportunities; grow contracted base. |
| 4. Commercial Expansion Readiness | مرحلة الجاهزية للتوسع التجاري | 24–36 months | Prepare Wave 3 and scaling of validated channels. |

### Revenue Channel Sequencing Table — تسلسل تفعيل القنوات

| Wave | Window | Channels | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wave 1 | 0–12 months | Operating & Governance Fees; Field Services & Reporting | Recurring anchor + relatively early income. |
| Wave 2 | 12–24 months | Exploration & Analysis Programs; Data & Remote Sensing | Program and data demand once capability is proven. |
| Wave 3 | 24–36 months | Field Hardware & Computing Integration | Requires the field-integration layer built in Year 2. |

### Phase Priorities

- **First 12-month priorities:** stand up commercial capability; position and validate Wave 1 channels; issue first proposals; build the pipeline register.
- **12–24 month priorities:** validate and begin contracting Wave 2 channels; deepen the pipeline; confirm pricing bases.
- **24–36 month priorities:** prepare Wave 3 integration channel; scale validated channels; establish expansion readiness.

### Required Operating Readiness and Capacity

Commercial activation depends on operating readiness from the Use of Funds deployment: the team (Operations & Talent), the data/compute base (Local Data & Computing), the field-integration layer (built Year 2), and the governance/reporting fabric. Partner-dependent channels (data sourcing, hardware/compute) require **PROPOSED** arrangements to be confirmed before Wave 2/3 activation.

### Sales and Proposal Workflow — مسار المبيعات والعروض

Identify → qualify → discuss → propose (Service Proposal, Document 06) → contract review (G4) → active. Each step is logged in the pipeline register (Document 02) and gated by the validation plan (Document 04); external communication follows the single-voice rule (G5).

### Relationship to the 15-Year Model

The roadmap operationalizes the model's revenue ramp: Years 1–3 target roughly **20 / 40 / 60%** of plateau, i.e. ~SAR 47.7m / 95.4m / 143.1m (illustrative). Wave sequencing is designed so that channel activation tracks the ramp and feeds the operating break-even and revenue-validation milestones.
