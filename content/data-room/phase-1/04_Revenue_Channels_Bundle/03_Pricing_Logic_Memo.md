### Document Purpose

This memo explains the pricing logic (منطق التسعير) for each of the five revenue channels — the principles, the pricing models, the cost and value drivers, the pricing units, payment structures, and review triggers. It sets out **logic and illustrative units**, because contracted pricing per channel is **[To be defined]**. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; nothing here is a price list or a commitment.**

---

### Executive Summary

Pricing follows a small set of principles applied per channel: recurring channels are priced on a **platform-plus-usage** basis; project channels on a **per-program / per-unit** basis; hybrid channels combine subscription with per-package or per-mission pricing. Across channels, price reflects both **cost drivers** (delivery cost, given channel margins of 30–55%) and **value drivers** (the decision-protection and capability value to the client). Illustrative pricing units are drawn from the model as working assumptions; final pricing is set through the review process and remains **[To be defined]**.

### Pricing Principles — مبادئ التسعير

1. **Transparency:** pricing methodology is explicit, consistent with the ecosystem's claims discipline.
2. **Value and cost together:** price reflects client value while covering cost at the channel's target margin.
3. **Unit clarity:** each channel has a defined pricing unit.
4. **Review-driven:** prices are set and revised through a defined review process, not ad hoc.
5. **Illustrative until validated:** all units are working assumptions; contracted pricing is **[To be defined]**.

### Value-Based, Cost-Plus, and Other Logics

- **Value-based pricing logic:** anchors price to the decision-protection and capability value delivered (strongest for operating/governance and data/analysis).
- **Cost-plus pricing logic:** sets price above delivery cost at the channel margin (relevant for hardware-weighted integration, 30% margin).
- **Project-based pricing:** per-program or per-project fees (exploration programs; integration projects).
- **Recurring fee logic:** platform and per-active-unit fees (operating & governance).
- **Milestone-based pricing:** payment tied to delivery milestones (programs and integration).
- **Field-service pricing:** per-mission and per-deployment fees plus reporting (field services).
- **Data and analysis pricing:** subscription plus per-scene/per-package (data & remote sensing).
- **Governance and reporting fee logic:** recurring fees for continuous operation and reporting.

### Pricing Review Process — عملية مراجعة التسعير

Pricing is proposed by the commercial owner, reviewed by FAL (financial) against target margins, checked by RCL for concentration/discount risk, and approved per the authority matrix before issue. **Review triggers:** cost-driver change, margin drift, scenario shift (Base→Conservative), competitive/market change, or a defined review interval.
