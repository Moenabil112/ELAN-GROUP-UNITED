### Document Purpose

This document provides **first-draft outline templates** for the commercial instruments the window uses to propose, contract, order, and deliver services across the revenue channels. They are drafting scaffolds with placeholders, not executable agreements. **Every template is a non-binding draft requiring independent legal review [Professional Review] before any use; all figures are illustrative and no contract is claimed.**

---

### Executive Summary

Six templates cover the commercial lifecycle: a Service Proposal, a Master Services Agreement outline, a Service Order, a Field Service Order, a Data and Analysis Deliverable, and a Governance Reporting Deliverable. Each follows a common structure — purpose, client, scope, deliverables, timeline, pricing, payment terms, review, acceptance, and signature — so that instruments are consistent and reviewable. All operative terms are placeholders; legal, pricing, and payment terms are completed only through professional review and the pricing process (Document 03).
