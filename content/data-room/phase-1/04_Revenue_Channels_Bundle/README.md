### Purpose of the Package

This bundle explains **how the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية) converts operating capability into verifiable revenue paths**. It maps the five revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد), provides the pipeline register, pricing logic, validation plan, 36-month commercial roadmap, and first-draft commercial templates. It is prepared for readability by commercial and financial reviewers and for conversion into Word or PDF. **All channels are verifiable, not contracted; all figures are illustrative and model-derived; contracted pricing is [To be defined] and no signed contract is claimed.**

### Relationship to the 15-Year Financial Model

The five channels are the revenue side of the 15-year model. Their plateau (~SAR 238.4m at Year 5), blended margin (~46%), and channel NPVs (~SAR 736m at 11%) are the model's revenue outputs. Channel activation is sequenced to the model's ramp (20 → 100% of plateau over Years 1–5), and validated channels feed the operating break-even (Year 1 base) and the revenue assumptions in the Financial Model Bundle.

### Relationship to Commercial Validation

No channel is treated as revenue until it clears the six-stage validation plan (Document 04) — market need, commercial interest, proposal, contract, delivery, and financial validation — and passes the relevant governance gates (G4 contracts; G6 partner where PROPOSED; G2 financial). Revenue recognition treatment is **[Professional Review]**.
