### Document Purpose

This is the master document of the revenue architecture of the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية). It defines the five approved revenue channels (مسارات الإيراد), what each sells, its revenue type, activation timeline, link to the 15-year financial model, operating and validation requirements, key risks, and governance checkpoint. **All channels are verifiable, not contracted; all figures are illustrative and model-derived; contracted pricing per channel is [To be defined] and no signed contract is claimed.**

---

### Executive Summary

The window earns through five channels, each built on the operating capability the SAR 100 million deployment creates. At plateau (Year 5, illustrative), the five channels total **SAR 238.4 million** in annual revenue at a blended contribution margin of **~46%**, and a total channel NPV @ 11% of **~SAR 736 million**. The channels differ in revenue type — recurring, project-based, and hybrid — and in activation timing, sequenced so that recurring and early-income channels prove first and capability-dependent channels follow. Every channel is treated as a path to be **validated**, not a booked contract.

### 1. Operating and Governance Fees — رسوم التشغيل والحوكمة

- **Definition:** Recurring fees for operating the mining-intelligence and governance platform on an ongoing basis.
- **What the client pays for:** Continuous operation and governance of the platform (a platform operation fee plus per-active-unit governance fees).
- **Revenue type:** Recurring.
- **Activation timeline:** Wave 1 (0–12 months) — the recurring anchor.
- **Model link:** Sovereign operating channel; plateau ~117.5; margin 55%; largest NPV.
- **Operating requirements:** Governance platform live; reporting cadence operating.
- **Validation requirements:** Confirmed client demand for continuous operation; pricing basis.
- **Key risks:** Buyer concentration (dependency on the sovereign channel); adoption speed.
- **Governance checkpoint:** G4 (contracts); G2 (financial); G5 single voice for any external comms.

### 2. Exploration and Analysis Programs — برامج الاستكشاف والتحليل

- **Definition:** Structured, paid exploration and analysis programs delivered on a per-program basis.
- **What the client pays for:** Defined exploration programs and an analysis/data retainer.
- **Revenue type:** Project-based (with retainer component).
- **Activation timeline:** Wave 2 (12–24 months).
- **Model link:** Programmatic exploration channel; plateau ~36.2; margin 40%.
- **Operating requirements:** Analysis capability and program-delivery team.
- **Validation requirements:** Program demand; delivery capability; per-program pricing.
- **Key risks:** Execution uncertainty inherent to exploration; program pipeline depth.
- **Governance checkpoint:** G4 (contracts); G6 (partner) where partner-dependent.

### 3. Data and Remote Sensing — البيانات والاستشعار عن بعد

- **Definition:** Interpretation of geological, spatial, and remote-sensing data as subscriptions and per-package deliverables.
- **What the client pays for:** Regional tasking subscriptions plus per-scene analysis packages.
- **Revenue type:** Hybrid (subscription + per-unit).
- **Activation timeline:** Wave 2 (12–24 months).
- **Model link:** Data / remote-sensing channel; plateau ~18.1; margin 45%.
- **Operating requirements:** Data environment; analysis pipeline; sourcing (partner pipeline PROPOSED).
- **Validation requirements:** Data demand; sourcing arrangement; pricing per unit.
- **Key risks:** Dependence on a proposed remote-sensing pipeline; data-quality risk.
- **Governance checkpoint:** G6 (partner, PROPOSED); OG-11 (sensitive-data sharing); G4 (contracts).

### 4. Field Hardware and Computing Integration — تكامل الأجهزة والحوسبة الميدانية

- **Definition:** Programming and integration of field hardware and edge computing at client sites.
- **What the client pays for:** Edge units programmed plus integration and support services.
- **Revenue type:** Project-based.
- **Activation timeline:** Wave 3 (24–36 months) — after field integration is built (Year 2).
- **Model link:** Edge-compute integration channel; plateau ~34.5; margin 30% (lowest, hardware-weighted).
- **Operating requirements:** Field-integration layer live; edge-compute capability (supply loop PROPOSED).
- **Validation requirements:** Integration demand; delivery capability; supply arrangement.
- **Key risks:** Dependence on a proposed hardware/compute loop; integration complexity.
- **Governance checkpoint:** G6 (partner, PROPOSED); G4 (contracts); asset gate.

### 5. Field Services and Reporting — الخدمات الميدانية والتقارير

- **Definition:** Field surveys, deployments, sampling coordination, and periodic reporting.
- **What the client pays for:** Drone survey missions, robotic deployments, and annual analysis/reporting packages.
- **Revenue type:** Hybrid (recurring reporting + per-mission).
- **Activation timeline:** Wave 1 (0–12 months) — relatively early income.
- **Model link:** Field services channel; plateau ~32.1; margin 35%.
- **Operating requirements:** Field-integration capability; reporting workflows.
- **Validation requirements:** Service demand; field readiness; per-mission pricing.
- **Key risks:** Field-execution uncertainty; capacity constraints.
- **Governance checkpoint:** G4 (contracts); field go-live (G9); G2 (financial).
