### Document Purpose

This document is the structured register for tracking commercial opportunities (سجل الفرص التجارية) across the five revenue channels. It defines the opportunity categories, stages, evidence, and review cadence so that the pipeline is governed and auditable rather than anecdotal. **All entries are illustrative placeholders; no opportunity is a booked contract, and all figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

The commercial pipeline is the disciplined record of how the window moves opportunities from identification to active revenue. Each opportunity is tagged to a revenue channel (مسارات الإيراد), a target client type, a stage, a probability, an estimated annual value, an expected activation date, a next action, an owner, and the evidence required to advance. The register uses six stages — Identified, Qualified, Under Discussion, Proposal Stage, Contract Review, and Active — and is reviewed on a fixed cadence, feeding the revenue-validation plan (Document 04).

### Purpose of the Commercial Pipeline

The pipeline exists to make commercial progress measurable and reviewable. It converts scattered conversations into a governed register that shows, at any time, what is real, what stage it is at, what evidence supports it, and what must happen next — and it feeds the validation and roadmap documents with grounded inputs rather than assumptions.
