### Document Purpose

This plan explains how each revenue channel is **validated (تحقق مسارات الإيراد)** — the stages an opportunity passes through, the evidence required at each, and the governance approvals that move a channel from claim to confirmed revenue. It is the discipline that lets the window state a channel is real. **All channels are verifiable, not contracted; revenue recognition treatment is [Professional Review]; all figures are illustrative and model-derived.**

---

### Executive Summary

Revenue validation converts a modeled channel into a confirmed one through six stages: Market Need Confirmation, Commercial Interest, Proposal Validation, Contractual Validation, Delivery Validation, and Financial Validation. Each stage has defined evidence, and each channel is tracked against the same matrix. A channel is only treated as revenue-recognition-ready once it clears contractual, delivery, and financial validation and passes the relevant governance gates. This plan is the bridge between the pipeline (Document 02) and revenue recognition (إثبات الإيراد).

### Purpose of Revenue Validation

Validation exists so that no channel is presented as revenue before it is real. It replaces optimism with evidence: demand, interest, proposal, contract, delivery capability, and financial confirmation — each documented — so that the model's illustrative channels become confirmed revenue paths only when they have earned that status.

### Evidence Types — أنواع الأدلة

- **Contract evidence:** executed contracts / service orders (G4).
- **Client demand evidence:** documented need and interest.
- **Pricing evidence:** confirmed pricing basis per channel.
- **Delivery capability evidence:** demonstrated ability to deliver to acceptance criteria.
- **Financial model evidence:** alignment of validated terms with the 15-year model assumptions.

### Revenue Recognition Readiness — جاهزية إثبات الإيراد

A channel is **revenue-recognition-ready (إثبات الإيراد)** only when it has cleared contractual, delivery, and financial validation and the accounting treatment has been confirmed under **[Professional Review]**. This document does not set accounting policy; it sets the readiness gate that precedes it.
