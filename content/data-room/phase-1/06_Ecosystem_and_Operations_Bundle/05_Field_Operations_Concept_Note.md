### Document Purpose

This concept note explains field operations (التشغيل الميداني) — the role of field teams and survey tools, field data capture and reporting, site-visit and sampling workflows, and how field activity connects to the financial dashboard and risk controls. **Field parameters are [Missing Input]; all figures are illustrative and model-derived; field go-live is gated (G9).**

---

### Executive Summary

Field operations are the ecosystem's connection to the ground — the Smart Field Operations layer (التشغيل الميداني الذكي, L3). Field teams, drones and survey tools, and sensors capture structured, governed evidence that flows into the intelligence and data layers and surfaces as dashboard indicators. The defining principle is **evidence discipline**: every field activity produces classified, access-tagged data with documented chain-of-custody, so field claims are auditable. Field operations feed the field-services revenue channel and reduce mining-execution risk by grounding decisions in captured evidence.

### Purpose of Field Operations

Field operations turn site activity into governed evidence. Their purpose is not merely to execute surveys and deployments but to produce **decision-grade, auditable data** — the raw material for exploration intelligence, dashboard indicators, and revenue validation.

### Field Activity Table — الأنشطة الميدانية

|---|---|---|
| Field team operations | On-site execution and coordination | OAL (field) |
| Drone & survey | Aerial survey and mapping missions | Field team |
| Sensing & measurement | Instrumented field measurement | Field team |
| Site visits | Scheduled, documented site visits | OAL |
| Sampling | Sample collection with chain-of-custody | Field team |
| Field reporting | Structured field reports | OAL / DKL |

### Role of Field Teams, Drones, and Survey Tools

Field teams execute and coordinate on-site work; drones and survey tools extend reach through aerial mapping; sensors provide instrumented measurement. Together they produce the telemetry (L3) that feeds anomaly detection and alerts — always under human accountability, with AI accelerating but never approving.

### Data Capture Table — التقاط البيانات

| Capture type | Output | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Field datasets | Structured site data | Classified on ingest |
| Survey outputs | Maps, survey records | Access-tagged |

| Samples | Sample records + chain-of-custody | Confidential where sensitive |

### Field Reporting and Workflows

- **Site-visit workflow:** scheduled → documented purpose → executed → recorded → reported.
- **Sample-documentation workflow:** collected → labeled → chain-of-custody → recorded → linked to analysis.
- **Field reporting:** structured reports assembled from captured evidence, deposited to the data room.

### Connection to Risk Controls

Field operations reduce **mining-execution risk** by grounding decisions in captured evidence and by gating field spend (field execution gate, G9). Field data quality is governed by the data layer (classification, access tags), reducing data-quality risk.

### Governance Checkpoint Table — نقاط التحقق الحوكمية

| Checkpoint | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Field go-live | G9 gate before field operations. |
| Asset acquisition | Asset gate (OAL). |
| Sensitive data | OG-11 for sensitive-data sharing. |
| Evidence discipline | Claims tied to sourced evidence; claims-lint. |

### Required Operating Evidence — الأدلة التشغيلية المطلوبة

Structured datasets, survey records, sensor logs, sample chain-of-custody, and field reports — all classified, access-tagged, and deposited to the data room.
