### Purpose of the Package

This bundle explains **what ELAN Group United builds operationally, how the ecosystem layers work, and how the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية) becomes an executable operating structure**. It sets out the operating model, the layer architecture, the stakeholder rooms, the 0–36 month roadmap, the field-operations concept, the technology architecture, and the data-to-decision workflow. It is prepared for readability by operating and investment reviewers and for conversion into Word or PDF. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; this describes an approved architecture in a pre-activation phase.**

### Relationship to the Local Operating Entry Window

The operating model makes the window **executable**: it defines the functions, layers, rooms, and workflow that turn the window from an investment concept into an operating structure. The window is the local operating layer of the ecosystem — contained within it, bounded from the protected IP core.

### Relationship to the 15-Year Financial Model

The operating model produces the activity the model measures: capability build (Use of Funds), channel activation (revenue ramp 20→100%), and operating break-even (Year 1 base). The financial cockpit (لوحة القرار المالي) renders the model's outputs from governed data.

### Relationship to Governance and Risk

Operations run entirely within the governance gates, decision rights, capital control, and risk register (Governance & Risk Bundle). Every material act follows the humans-decide/AI-accelerates workflow and is logged; nothing commits outside a gate.

### Relationship to Revenue Channels

The operating model is the precondition for the five revenue channels (Revenue Channels Bundle): the team, data, field, and governance layers must be built before operating fees, programs, data, integration, and field services can be activated and validated.
