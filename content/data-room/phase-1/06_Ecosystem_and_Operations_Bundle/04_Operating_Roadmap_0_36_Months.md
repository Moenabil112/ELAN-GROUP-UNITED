### Document Purpose

This roadmap sets out the first 36 months of execution — the operating objective, the phases from foundation to expansion readiness, the activation of governance/reporting, the data room and financial cockpit, the stakeholder review cadence, and the link to milestone-based capital release. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; each phase is gated — evidence before spend.**

---

### Executive Summary

The operating objective for the first 36 months is to move the Local Operating Entry Window from **establishment to validated, executable operation**. Five phases sequence this: Foundation, Operating Readiness, Revenue Validation, Field Execution Readiness, and Expansion Readiness. Governance, the data room, and the financial cockpit (لوحة القرار المالي) activate early so that every later phase is governed and measured. The roadmap aligns to the **40 / 60 staged capital release** and to the model's revenue ramp (20 → 60% of plateau across Years 1–3).

---

### Operating Reference

Phases: Foundation → Operating Readiness → Revenue Validation → Field Execution Readiness → Expansion Readiness · gates G1–G9 · capital 40 / 60 · model ramp 20/40/60% Y1–3.

---

### Operating Objective for the First 36 Months

To stand up the window's operating capability, activate governance and reporting, open the data room and cockpit, validate the first revenue channels, reach field-execution readiness, and prepare for expansion — each phase gated and evidenced.
