### Document Purpose

This document explains the layers (طبقات المنظومة) that make up the ELAN ecosystem — their purpose, inputs and outputs, responsible functions, financial-model link, risk-reduction role, required documents, and governance checkpoints. It covers the five core layers plus the data-room, stakeholder, and revenue-channel layers. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; the protected IP core is never transferred.**

---

### Executive Summary

The ecosystem is built as eight interacting layers: five core layers (Exploration Intelligence, Trust & Governance, Smart Field Operations, Investment & Revenue, Sustainability & Impact) and three cross-cutting layers (Data Room, Stakeholder, Revenue Channels). Each layer consumes defined inputs, produces defined outputs, and is governed at defined checkpoints. The **Trust & Governance Room (L2)** is the single source of truth that governs every other layer's claims; the **Investment & Revenue layer (L4)** holds the protected core that never crosses borders. Together the layers form a closed loop: operations feed intelligence, intelligence compounds capability in the core.
