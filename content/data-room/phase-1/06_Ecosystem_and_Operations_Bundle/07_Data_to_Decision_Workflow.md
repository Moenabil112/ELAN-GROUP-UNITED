### Document Purpose

This document explains how data becomes a governed decision (مسار تحويل البيانات إلى قرار) — the stages from capture to archiving, the inputs and outputs at each, who is responsible, and the decision evidence (أدلة القرار) produced. It is the operational expression of the ecosystem's central rule: humans decide, AI accelerates. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; AI never approves.**

---

### Executive Summary

Every material decision follows one governed path: data is captured, validated, analyzed, reviewed by governance, prepared as a recommendation, approved by an accountable human, reported to stakeholders, and archived as evidence. This eight-stage workflow is the operational form of the 12-step decision flow. Its discipline is that **no decision skips validation, governance review, or logging** — and that AI accelerates every stage but approves none. The workflow is what makes the ecosystem's decisions auditable and its claims defensible.

---

### Operating Reference

Central rule: humans decide, AI accelerates. Underlying flow: 12 steps (evidence → analysis → risk → financial → legal → recommendation → approval → log → assignment → monitoring). Task types: T-D intake · T-C review · T-A implementation · T-F gate · T-G reporting · T-H KB-update.

---

### Purpose of the Workflow

The workflow exists so that decisions rest on validated evidence and pass through governance before they are made — and so that every decision leaves an auditable trail. It converts raw data into governed decisions without ever letting automation make the call.

### Data Sources and Intake

- **Data sources:** field operations (L3), exploration intelligence (L1), financial and operational data, external inputs.
- **Data intake:** deposited via T-D (data-intake) tasks; classified on ingest; residency enforced (in-country).

### Governance Review, Approval, and Archiving

- **Governance review:** risk (RCL), financial (FAL), and legal (LCL) assessment before any recommendation is finalized.
- **Decision approval:** the accountable human approves per the authority matrix; **AI never approves**; T0 matters are human-only.
- **Reporting back:** the decision and its basis are reported to the relevant stakeholder rooms per RBAC and cadence.
- **Evidence archiving:** the decision is logged immutably (append-only) and its evidence deposited to the data room.
