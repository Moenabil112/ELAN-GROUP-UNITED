### Document Purpose

This is the master operating model (نموذج التشغيل) for ELAN Group United. It defines ELAN as a mining-intelligence operating structure, sets out its core operating functions and layers, its internal workflow and stakeholder-interaction model, and how the Local Operating Entry Window (نافذة التشغيل المحلية) relates to the broader ecosystem — and to the financial model, governance, and revenue channels. **All figures are illustrative and model-derived; this describes an approved architecture in a pre-activation phase.**

---

### Executive Summary

ELAN Group United is not a single company but a **national mining-intelligence operating system** — a layered structure that turns geological and operating data into governed decisions, protected intellectual property, and verifiable revenue. The **Local Operating Entry Window** is the local operating layer of that system, presented as an executable, investable unit. The operating model rests on one central rule: **humans decide, AI accelerates** — every material act moves from evidence to recommendation to a human gate to an immutable log to assigned execution and monitoring. This model is the connective tissue between the capital (SAR 100m), the 15-year financial model, the governance gates, and the five revenue channels.

### Definition: ELAN as a Mining-Intelligence Operating Structure

ELAN is a **mining-intelligence operating structure** — a system that produces exploration intelligence, governs it through a trust-and-data layer, executes smart field operations, protects value in an IP core, and measures sustainability impact. It is designed as a system, not a product: capability compounds in the protected core as operations feed it back.

### Relationship: Window and Broader Ecosystem

The Local Operating Entry Window is **contained within, but bounded from**, the ecosystem. It draws on the ecosystem's layered capability and data loop, while its participation, economics, and valuation are assessed on its own terms. The protected IP core sits outside the window and is never transferred.

### Internal Workflow

Every material act follows the same spine: **evidence → recommendation → human gate → immutable log → assigned execution → monitoring** (the 12-step decision flow, Document 07). No commitment is made outside a gate; AI never approves.

### Relationship to the 15-Year Financial Model

The operating model produces the activity the model measures: capability build (Use of Funds), channel activation (revenue ramp 20→100%), and the operating break-even (Year 1 base). The financial cockpit (لوحة القرار المالي) renders the model's outputs from governed data.

### Relationship to Governance and Risk Controls

The model operates entirely within the governance gates, decision rights, capital control, and risk register (Governance & Risk Bundle). Governance is not a wrapper around operations; it is built into every step.

### Relationship to Revenue Channel Activation

The operating model is what converts capability into the five revenue channels (Revenue Channels Bundle): the team, data, field, and governance layers are the preconditions for activating operating fees, programs, data, integration, and field services.
